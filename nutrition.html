Open Nutrition Data Commons for Nursing Education

A proposed 53-hour pathway where nursing learners use nutrition evidence, clinical and population data, visualization, and responsible AI to recognize risk, communicate clearly, and make appropriate referrals.

Pathway53 learner hours Program typeNursing education Working trackNIH Developing
North Star

Build for the nursing learner first

The challenge curriculum is a 53-hour nutrition pathway for nursing learners. The Health Data Analytics curriculum is a linked builder layer where data educators and learners can develop, test, document, and improve reusable activities.

A learner completing the pathway should be able to recognize nutrition risk, reason from clinical and population data, evaluate evidence, communicate within nursing scope, make an appropriate referral, and identify unsafe, biased, unsupported, or irrelevant AI-supported guidance.

Adoption test

Useful beyond Mass General Brigham

A nursing educator at another institution should be able to understand the pathway, inspect its competency and hour map, select a component for local use, and teach it without MGB credentials, protected health information, Azure access, or proprietary software.

  • Synthetic and open data
  • Platform independent
  • Auditable AI activities
  • Reusable faculty guidance

Evaluation

What a strong Developing Track entry must show

The working review model uses the eight published Curriculum Overview criteria, each scored from 0 to 5.

CriterionEvidence needed
FrameworkEvery claimed hour and competency traces to a lesson, learner action, and assessment.
InnovationData and AI change what learners can practice, inspect, and defend.
Teaching methodsAnother educator can see what learners and instructors do, in what order, and with what output.
Competency gapsA documented nursing nutrition gap maps directly to curriculum work.
Evaluation methodsMeasures cover knowledge, applied performance, communication, and responsible AI use.
Innovative approachesCases, simulation, data laboratories, visualization, and AI critique support learning outcomes.
Nursing education contextThe pathway has a credible learner level, calendar, approval path, and curriculum home.
Interdisciplinary approachNursing, nutrition, data education, and clinical AI contributors have defined responsibilities.
Internal review standard

Ready means evidence, not just a strong idea

The working go standard is at least 34 of 40 points, no criterion below 4, no unresolved administrative triage failure, and no unsupported institutional claim. This is an internal quality bar, not an NIH cutoff.
