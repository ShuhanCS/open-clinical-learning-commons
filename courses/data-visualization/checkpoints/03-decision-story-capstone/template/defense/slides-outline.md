# Defense slide outline

Export an accessible PDF named `slides.pdf`. Keep the presentation to eight minutes or less.

## Slide 1: audience and decision

Name both audiences, their authority, and the one requested decision.

## Slide 2: source and population

Name CMS, the public dataset, Massachusetts hospital population, selected facility, measures, reporting periods, and source release.

## Slide 3: finding

State the strongest historical finding without implying current performance or cause.

## Slide 4: primary executive figure

Explain the signal, time boundary, and authorization request.

## Slide 5: supporting technical figure

Explain the peer position, descriptive reference, mock trigger, and validation task.

## Slide 6: audience adaptation

Show what changed between versions and what remained invariant.

## Slide 7: material limitation

Explain the 590-day lag, public aggregation, and evidence needed before action.

## Slide 8: requested action and release checks

Name the owner, return evidence, next review, reproduction result, accessibility path, and AI verification.
