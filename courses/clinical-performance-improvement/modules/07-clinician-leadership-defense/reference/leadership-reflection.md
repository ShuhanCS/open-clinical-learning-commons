# Leadership reflection

The hardest leadership choice in this package is to resist converting partial improvement into readiness. S01 looks favorable on P90 wait and abandonment, and the ML challenger looks favorable on several error measures. Both still fail a declared conjunctive rule. The responsible decision is to keep those failures visible.

The analytic package can identify a bounded delay pattern, demand range, scenario tradeoffs, and missing evidence. It cannot assign root cause, determine safe staffing, measure actual burden, or authorize a clinical workflow. Leadership adds ownership and judgment, but it does not create evidence that the data did not contain.

My recommendation is `revise before testing`. I would keep no-change monitoring, require a new scenario contract, and ask clinical, safety, access, workforce, operations, and data owners to close or explicitly refer the open conditions. I would also preserve dissent from staff or reviewers who believe the learning aim, burden, or safety protections remain inadequate.

I remain accountable for the accuracy of the evidence synthesis, the recommendation boundary, the accessible communication, and the agent-use record. The clinical performance council remains accountable for any future decision to review, refer, stop, or separately authorize a protocol.
