# Revision and learning plan

- Current recommendation: `revise before testing`
- Current selected scenario: `none`
- Revision owner: `clinical performance council with clinical, operations, safety, access, and workforce leads`
- Current authorization: `evidence collection and curriculum design only`

## Stage 1: Close evidence and ownership gaps

Confirm current definitions, workflow, role coverage, safety review, access routes, workforce burden, follow-up completeness, data stewardship, and decision ownership. Record disagreements and unresolved limits.

## Stage 2: Write a new scenario contract

A revised option must define its mechanism, eligibility, exclusions, clinical overrides, resource inputs, schedule, fallback, balancing measures, access protections, workforce safeguards, data requirements, and predeclared decision rules. It cannot inherit a favorable status from S01 or S03.

## Stage 3: Repeat governed assumption testing

Use the accepted forecast or a newly approved forecast release, common random inputs, declared conditions, conservation checks, sensitivity analysis, and complete failure retention. Do not lower a rule after seeing the result.

## Stage 4: Repeat feasibility and monitoring review

Screen all required domains, define owned prospective measures, confirm unavailable states, review dashboard and communication routes, and retain human escalation, no-change fallback, and restart evidence.

## Stage 5: Return for leadership decision

The council may then revise, refer, stop, or consider a separately authorized bounded test protocol. This plan does not authorize that protocol now.
