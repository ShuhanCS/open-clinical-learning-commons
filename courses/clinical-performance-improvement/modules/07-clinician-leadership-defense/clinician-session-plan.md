# Clinician-led session plan

## Session 1: Clinical evidence conference, 4 hours

Joe Joseph, MD, or the approved clinician lead challenges the learner to explain the signal, safety, bottleneck, forecast, scenario, feasibility, monitoring, and ML evidence as one bounded clinical performance decision. The learner must name the strongest evidence, the largest uncertainty, and every action boundary.

## Session 2: People, workflow, and ownership lab, 4 hours

The clinician lead tests blame-free framing, clinical and safety implications, staff burden, access, equity, workforce limits, accountable ownership, and disagreement routes. The learner maps who may benefit, carry work, be missed, or have authority to pause the work.

## Session 3: Recommendation and stewardship studio, 4 hours

The clinician lead reviews the `revise before testing` recommendation, alternatives, local evidence gaps, revision path, measure and model stewardship, monitoring, escalation, fallback, and communication. No current scenario may be converted into a test protocol.

## Session 4: Leadership council defense, 4 hours

The clinician lead chairs a 14-question defense with clinical, safety, operations, access, workforce, methods, and governance challenges. The panel records conditions, disagreement, package status, recommendation, and final-checkpoint permission separately.

If the designated clinician is unavailable, the program may use an approved recorded case conference plus a qualified clinician-led synchronous defense that preserves the same evidence, questions, challenge standard, and feedback.
