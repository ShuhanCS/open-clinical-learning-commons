# Subgroup support interpretation

- Full-release supported groups: `3 of 3`
- Target-window supported groups: `1 of 3`
- Target-window language-support encounters: `401, not supported`
- Target-window mobility-support encounters: `242, not supported`
- Target-window standard encounters: `2,308, supported`
- Teaching support threshold: `1,000 encounters per group and window`
- Comparative target-window claim: `not supported`
- Owner: `CGH-ED-01 equity and access lead`

All three synthetic access-support groups have full-release support. That support cannot be borrowed for a narrower time-window comparison. In Weeks 35 through 44 evening shifts, language-support and mobility-support groups fall below the predeclared threshold. The release can report their counts and unavailable clocks, but it cannot compare their target-window rates or medians as a supported disparity analysis.

The standard group alone clearing the threshold does not make a cross-group comparison valid. Generated support categories do not describe real identities, prevalence, inequity, discrimination, or cause.
