This module awards no course points. It is a required readiness gateway to the 20-point Module 02 measure build and the 20-point Module 03 performance diagnostic that together form the 40-point Week 3 checkpoint.

| Criterion | Readiness points | Pass evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Decision, owner, and bounded next action | 4 | one decision owner, one construction action, and no clinical action |
| Unit of flow and process boundary | 4 | ordered encounter states, entry, exit, clocks, and explicit exclusions |

| Accountability and responsible claims | 4 | decision rights, affected groups, public-source limits, and synthetic separation are explicit |
| Reproducibility and progression | 4 | source identities, AI record, manifest, gates, and owned conditions validate |
| **Total** | **20** | **16 points or more and every gate passes or passes with an owned condition** |

Readiness points document preparation only. They are not course points and must not be added to the 100-point course ledger.

## Noncompensable gates

1. The service is explicitly fictional.
2. The unit of flow is one synthetic adult emergency encounter.
3. Public hospital aggregates are not represented as local operations.
4. All three accepted full sources have exact identity records.
5. Unavailable and sentinel values are preserved.
6. Entry, exit, clocks, and excluded scope are defined.
7. Measure families include process, outcome, balancing, safety, capacity, access, and equity roles.
8. One accountable decision owner and one Module 02 action are named.
9. No bottleneck diagnosis appears.
10. No staffing proposal or clinical action appears.
11. No hospital ranking, causal, or public-to-synthetic linkage claim appears.
12. The package is complete, portable, disclosed, and reproducible.

Allowed progression values are `continue`, `continue with conditions`, `revise`, and `refer`. Only the first two permit Module 02 curriculum construction.
