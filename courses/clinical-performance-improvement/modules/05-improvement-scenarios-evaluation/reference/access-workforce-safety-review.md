# Access, workforce, and safety review

- Language-support gap rule: `no more than 5 minutes worse`
- S01 point-demand language-gap worsening: `0.013189 minutes`
- S02 point-demand language-gap worsening: `-0.345440 minutes`
- S03 point-demand language-gap worsening: `0.031209 minutes`
- S01 point-demand flex requirement: `40.000000 clinician-hours`
- S03 point-demand flex requirement: `25.220413 clinician-hours`
- Safety outcome: `not simulated; prospective measurement required`
- Return within 72 hours: `not simulated; prospective measurement required`

All three options keep the modeled language-support gap inside the predeclared bound, but the model does not establish equitable access. Mobility-support effects, unavailable states, and group support must remain visible. Flex hours are modeled resources, not staffing recommendations. Workforce interruptions and perceived load have no accepted baseline and must be measured before a test.

No favorable safety or return claim is permitted. A real test would need prospective clinical review, safety-event capture, follow-up completeness, and stop rules.
