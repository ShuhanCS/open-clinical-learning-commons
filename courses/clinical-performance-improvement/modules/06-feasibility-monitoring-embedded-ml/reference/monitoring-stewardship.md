The flow, access, safety, quality, workforce, clinical operations, and data owners review their measures in the declared cadence. Leadership reviews measures together so a gain in one area cannot hide harm or burden elsewhere. Source drift, denominator failure, missing groups, and follow-up incompleteness are recorded before interpretation.
