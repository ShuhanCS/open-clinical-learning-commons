# Gaming and unintended effects

The prospective plan must look for these failure modes:

1. delayed or altered timestamps that make waiting appear shorter;
2. routing low-acuity patients outside the declared rule;
3. excluding left-before-seen encounters from the denominator;
4. shifting work to nurses, support staff, or later hours without recording burden;
5. faster completion paired with worse high-acuity waits;
6. reduced reporting or follow-up that makes safety and returns look favorable;
7. uneven adoption that hides crossover and contamination; and
8. access-support groups disappearing into an unavailable aggregate.

The response is to preserve event order, denominators, routing eligibility, activation, adherence, workload, and unavailable states. A favorable dashboard is not sufficient when the process producing the measure has changed.
