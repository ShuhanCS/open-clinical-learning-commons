# Subgroup support interpretation

The synthetic access-support groups have 6,032 language-support encounters, 3,498 mobility-support encounters, and 34,098 standard encounters. Each group clears the teaching threshold of 1,000 accepted encounters. The calculated medians and left-before-seen rates can therefore be carried into Module 03 as supported synthetic comparisons.

The group labels test whether a measure system preserves access needs. They do not represent real people, prevalence, disadvantage, discrimination, or causal effects. Any later comparison must show denominators, unavailable clocks, and the generated-data boundary.
