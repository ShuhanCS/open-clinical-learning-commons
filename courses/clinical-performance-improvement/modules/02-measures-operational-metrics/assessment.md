# Assessment contract

Module 02 awards 20 of the 40 Week 3 checkpoint points. Module 03 awards the other 20 points.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Measure definitions and denominators | 4 |
| Event logic and data repair | 4 |
| Operational metric construction | 4 |
| Validation and responsible interpretation | 4 |
| Reproducibility and progression | 4 |
| **Total** | **20** |

Full credit requires 16 or more points and all 15 noncompensable gates. A numeric score cannot compensate for an invalid denominator, public-to-synthetic linkage, failed event or conservation check, missing safety or subgroup support, incomplete disclosure, or an unauthorized diagnosis or action.

The required submission is:

1. four executable SQL files;
2. ten completed learner records;
3. eight reproduced measure outputs plus the build report;
4. a 20-point score and 15 gate results; and
5. one progression decision with seven owned Module 03 conditions.
