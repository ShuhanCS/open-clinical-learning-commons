# Clinical performance and improvement decision charter

- Decision owner: `CGH-ED-01 clinical performance and improvement council`
- Decision: `Are the service problem, unit of flow, source evidence, measure families, and accountable action defined well enough to build the local measurement system?`
- Proposed next action: `build and validate local measure definitions from the fictional service event model in Module 02`
- Service: `CGH-ED-01`
- Service status: `fictional adult emergency service with no public hospital linkage`
- Unit of flow: `one synthetic adult emergency encounter from recorded arrival through recorded departure`
- Entry: `first valid recorded emergency-service arrival for the encounter`
- Exit: `recorded departure after discharge, admission, transfer, or other documented disposition`
- Problem statement: `the council needs a defensible way to measure delay, access, safety, demand, capacity, and burden before it can test whether performance should change`
- Target population: `synthetic adults age 18 or older with a qualifying CGH-ED-01 encounter`
- Public source role: `define public measure families, reveal source availability, and provide historical aggregate context`
- Future local evidence: `synthetic encounter, process-event, staffing, queue-snapshot, safety-event, calendar-demand, scenario, and known-truth tables beginning in Module 02`
- Measure families: `timeliness, access, process reliability, outcome, balancing, safety, demand, capacity, workload, and equity`
- Evidence standard: `exact source identity, explicit grain, reproducible event logic, conserved denominators, visible unavailable values, accountable ownership, and no unsupported local or causal claim`
- Accountability: `the council owns progression; faculty and clinician reviewers own method and clinical-boundary review; patient/access representatives review burden and supported subgroup meaning`
- Claim boundary: `public aggregate evidence supports definitions and feasibility only; synthetic local evidence may support teaching decisions only and never a claim about a real hospital`
- Stop or referral trigger: `changed source identity, public-to-synthetic linkage, missing unit or clock, unavailable values treated as observed, patient inference, operational diagnosis, staffing proposal, hospital ranking, causal claim, or clinical action`
- Operational diagnosis: `prohibited in Module 01`
- Staffing change: `prohibited in Module 01`
- Clinical action: `prohibited`
- Implementation status: `not authorized`
- Module 02 handoff: `construct versioned local measure specifications against the declared fictional event model`

## Why this decision matters

Performance improvement can fail before any analysis begins if the service boundary, unit, clock, denominator, or accountable action is vague. This charter forces those choices into the open. It also prevents public reporting data from being mistaken for current local operations.

## What Module 02 must build

Module 02 must instantiate the fictional event model, preserve source and derived fields, write exact numerator and denominator rules, test exclusions and missing states, separate event time from reporting time, and release the 20-point measure build. It must not yet diagnose a bottleneck or propose staffing.
