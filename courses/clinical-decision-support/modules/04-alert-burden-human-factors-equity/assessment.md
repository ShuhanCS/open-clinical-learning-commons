# Assessment contract

Module 04 contributes 25 course points once at the Week 6 checkpoint. A point total cannot compensate for a failed gate.

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Workflow task analysis | 3.00 |
| Roles, handoffs, timing, and interruption | 2.50 |
| Six-candidate comparison | 3.00 |
| Burden and alert-budget review | 3.00 |
| Passive-panel and no-alert comparison | 3.00 |
| Usability and human-factors review | 2.50 |
| Automation-bias, override, and stop controls | 2.00 |
| Access, equity, and privacy review | 3.00 |
| Patient communication and hidden work | 1.50 |
| Reproduction, AI record, decision, and handoff | 1.50 |
| Total | 25.00 |

The reference progression requires 25.00 of 25.00 and all 20 gates. A passing learner submission may receive another score under the grading rubric, but Module 05 cannot begin while any noncompensable gate fails.

The gates protect the Week 3 chain of custody, all six unaccepted candidates, rejection of the `0.20` fixture, synthetic-only use, task and handoff analysis, burden metrics, repeat and unavailable states, interaction interpretation, passive-panel and no-alert comparisons, usability, automation-bias controls, access, equity, privacy, patient communication, human override, reproduction, AI disclosure, progression, and the no-clinical-authority boundary.

The module decision is a sandbox decision. It is not a clinical recommendation, threshold selection, silent-mode approval, implementation approval, or deployment decision.
