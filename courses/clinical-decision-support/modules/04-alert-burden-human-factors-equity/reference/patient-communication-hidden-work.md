# Patient communication and hidden work

## Communication contract

The clinician owns any conversation. The panel must say that it is a nonproduction teaching concept, the score is synthetic, the threshold is unaccepted, one HbA1c result is not a diagnosis, and no action is automatic. A patient may ask questions, decline discussion, request a qualified interpreter or accessible format, and report a concern without penalty.

Spanish and other-language routes require qualified language support. The screen-reader, low-vision, motor-access, and cognitive-support routes require explicit testing. The synthetic access labels are test prompts, not diagnoses or claims about a person.

## Hidden work register

- noticing or deciding not to open the passive panel;
- checking whether inputs are ready and explaining an unavailable state;
- reviewing uncertainty and determining whether discussion belongs in the encounter;
- arranging qualified language or accessible-format support;
- explaining purpose, limits, nonaction, privacy, and recourse;
- documenting a concern without converting the panel into an order;
- handling repeat exposure, deferment, or unresolved work; and
- reporting a defect or asking governance to pause the concept.

The release scripts 20.00 task minutes across 12 candidate cards at `0.03`. That value is an assumption, not measured labor, cost, or capacity. Faculty should require sensitivity analysis at half and twice the scripted minutes.

No patient communication record authorizes contact, targeting, outreach, testing, diagnosis, treatment, or follow-up.
