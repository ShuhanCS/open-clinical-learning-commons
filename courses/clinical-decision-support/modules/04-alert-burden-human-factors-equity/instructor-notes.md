# Instructor notes

This module is a workflow-design exercise, not an alert trial. Keep the public NHANES evidence separate from the synthetic service script. The historical missed-case and flag rates do not predict local burden. The synthetic card counts do not establish local prevalence, performance, benefit, behavior, or harm.

The reference recommends `panel-t003` only because it supplies 12 positive synthetic cases for Module 05 mechanics without scripted interruption. The `0.03` value remains unaccepted. It cannot be presented as a clinical threshold, silent-mode threshold, local policy, or patient-level recommendation.

Require learners to explain all six threshold candidates. At `0.075` and `0.10`, the synthetic workflow produces no cards while the NHANES temporal holdout still contains missed historical events. That mismatch is a reason for caution, not evidence that either source is wrong.

A scripted dismissal is an interaction state. It does not reveal motivation, fatigue, misuse, or care quality. Scripted response time and task minutes are assumptions. Learners should test how conclusions change when those assumptions change rather than treating them as measurements.

The equity file withholds most `0.03` candidate-card rates because the support rule is not met. Do not accept filled cells, merged groups, group ranking, fairness certification, or targeting proposals. The supported English-language slice does not resolve Spanish, other-language, disability-access, age, gender, or BMI questions.

Progression permits only Module 05 sandbox construction. No learner package may score real patients, display a clinical alert, make a care recommendation, connect to a live system, begin silent-mode evaluation, implement, or deploy.
