# Instructor notes

## Teaching purpose

This module tests whether learners can make and defend a bounded leadership recommendation from an imperfect CDS evidence chain. The work is interpretation, ownership, communication, and governance. It is not another opportunity to improve the model or prototype.

## Review order

1. Verify both checkpoint identities and the generated release manifest.
2. Read the product brief and recommendation before reviewing the score.
3. Confirm that the 40-point and 25-point checkpoints remain unscored here.
4. Check the blocked accessibility defect, silent failure, threshold status, and R03/R04/R08 failures.
5. Ask all 14 defense questions and require an accepted evidence path, practical consequence, and limit for every answer.
6. Record curriculum-package status and CDS recommendation separately.

## Clinician boundary

Joe Joseph, MD, SFHM, is designated under dated public sources. Do not claim that he reviewed, endorsed, or taught the package until direct confirmation exists. A qualified substitute route must preserve the same clinician-led challenge and defense standard.

## Release boundary

The reference is `accept with conditions` as a curriculum package and `revise before seeking local silent-mode approval` as a CDS recommendation. No learner may repair the frozen candidate, accept a threshold, waive a failure, or grant evaluation, clinical, implementation, production, or deployment authority.
