# Leadership safety case

- Disposition: `revise before seeking local silent-mode approval`.
- Hazards: `22 of 22 retained`.
- Inherited sandbox failures: `17 of 17 retained`.
- Monitoring measures: `20 of 20 retained`.
- Escalation routes: `12 of 12 human owned`.
- Automatic clinical actions: `0`.
- Accessibility blocker: `one malformed card remains blocked`.
- Silent-failure route: `four independent ledgers`.
- Safe current fallback: `no clinical use and no real-patient scoring`.

Every hazard retains its cause, patient or user consequence, detection route, control, owner, escalation, fallback, stop rule, restart rule, retirement trigger, and claim limit. This completeness supports leadership review. It does not prove that the concept is safe in care.

The governance council must stop or refer the proposal if a failure is hidden, a patient or staff consequence lacks an owner, reconciliation is unavailable, the accessible equivalent fails, a threshold appears without approval, clinical action becomes automatic, or authority expands beyond curriculum construction.

Restart requires documented resolution of the triggering condition, independent verification, accountable clinical and safety review, updated accessible evidence, and a new governance decision. Retirement remains available when the intended use no longer has a defensible benefit, ownership, evidence path, safe fallback, or maintainable interface.
