## Scope of a later proposal

A later governance package may request review of a bounded silent-mode protocol. It must specify duration, population, data minimization, no-care-action controls, reconciliation, safety oversight, patient and staff protections, analysis, stopping, reporting, and retirement. This curriculum record does not submit or approve that protocol.

## Prohibited interpretation

The proposal is not permission to connect a production system, score real patients, start silent mode, display a card, recommend testing, change care, or deploy. A future local council must make any such decision under its own authority.
