# Logic, input, and threshold record

## Logic boundary

The advisory remains nonbinding. It may ask the clinician to consider confirmatory HbA1c testing after required information is available and before encounter close. It may be suppressed, dismissed, or followed by no action. None of those states automatically indicates error or low risk.

The accepted logic contracts retain eligibility, encounter context, information cutoff, trigger, suppression, recent-result handling, known-condition handling, units, terminology, duplicate handling, version checks, delayed and unavailable inputs, and ordered traces. No leadership record changes them.

## Input boundary

The public NHANES release supplies historical modeling evidence. Synthetic Synthea and Commons records supply event-time input availability, mechanics, workflow, burden, trace, drift, interaction, and failure truth. No public record is linked to a synthetic person. No real patient record enters the package.

FHIR R4-shaped resources and CDS Hooks-shaped requests and responses remain teaching shapes. Their passing mechanics do not establish conformance or production readiness.

## Threshold boundary

- Evidence candidates: `0.020, 0.030, 0.040, 0.050, 0.075, and 0.100`.
- Candidate status: all unselected and unaccepted.
- Module 02 value: `0.20`, rejected mechanics fixture.
- Sandbox value: `0.03000000`, unaccepted mechanics fixture.
- Accepted clinical threshold: `none`.
- Agent threshold selection: prohibited.

A future threshold decision requires local clinical purpose, outcome, burden, missed-case, access, workflow, calibration, subgroup-support, safety, governance, and evaluation evidence. Module 07 does not make that decision.
