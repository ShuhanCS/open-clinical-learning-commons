# Technical defense

## Q01. What exact decision does this package support?

- Exact answer: `accept with conditions` for the curriculum package and `revise before seeking local silent-mode approval` for the CDS recommendation.
- Evidence: `recommendation-and-alternatives.md` and `progression-decision.md`.
- Decision consequence: the candidate may enter final-checkpoint curriculum review, but it may not enter a local evaluation approval process yet.
- Limit: curriculum completeness does not grant clinical or evaluation authority.

## Q02. What is the strongest evidence and the largest uncertainty?

- Exact answer: the strongest evidence is the exact reproducible chain across both checkpoints; the largest uncertainty is translation from historical and synthetic teaching evidence to a locally owned clinical purpose, threshold, workflow, and safety protocol.
- Evidence: `evidence-synthesis.md` and `reproducibility-check.md`.
- Decision consequence: preserve the package and require local revision evidence.
- Limit: reproducibility does not establish local benefit or safety.

## Q03. What intended action and prohibited action remain fixed?

- Exact answer: a clinician may review the current record and consider confirmatory HbA1c testing, defer, dismiss, or take no action; diagnosis, automatic ordering, blocked workflow, treatment change, denial, targeting, and action without clinician review are prohibited.
- Evidence: `product-brief.md` and `logic-input-threshold.md`.
- Decision consequence: any expanded action requires a new intended-use and governance review.
- Limit: the current package does not recommend a test for any real patient.

## Q04. Why is there no accepted threshold?

- Exact answer: all six historical candidates remain unselected and unaccepted; `0.20` is rejected mechanics and `0.03000000` is an unaccepted sandbox fixture.
- Evidence: `logic-input-threshold.md` and `evidence/checkpoint1/logic-evidence-readiness-review.md`.
- Decision consequence: do not seek silent-mode approval until a local threshold and consequence review is complete.
- Limit: a historical probability or candidate cut point cannot become a clinical decision by reuse.

## Q05. What did the sandbox establish and fail to establish?

- Exact answer: 31 cases produced their declared local mechanics behavior, including visible stops and silent-failure detection.
- Evidence: `prototype-disclosure.md` and `evidence/checkpoint2/responsible-claims-check.md`.
- Decision consequence: use the cases to teach failure handling and revision requirements.
- Limit: the sandbox does not establish interoperability, accessibility, usability, safety, local validity, or utility.

## Q06. Which failure matters most for the current recommendation?

- Exact answer: the blocked malformed-card accessibility defect prevents an approval-ready interface; the silent failure also requires local independent reconciliation and stop ownership.
- Evidence: `prototype-disclosure.md`, `safety-case.md`, and `monitoring-silent-failure-plan.md`.
- Decision consequence: both routes remain explicit revision conditions.
- Limit: one seeded silent event does not estimate a clinical rate.

## Q07. What does the safety case support?

- Exact answer: all 22 hazards have detection, controls, owners, escalation, fallback, stop, restart, and retirement routes.
- Evidence: `safety-case.md` and `evidence/checkpoint2/candidate/module-06/safety-case.md`.
- Decision consequence: the teaching case is organized enough for leadership review.
- Limit: a complete teaching safety case does not prove local safety.

## Q08. How are silent and unavailable states governed?

- Exact answer: silence is reconciled from request, response, terminal-trace, and human-notice ledgers; unavailable reconciliation stops the affected route.
- Evidence: `monitoring-silent-failure-plan.md`.
- Decision consequence: absence from one service log cannot be treated as success.
- Limit: unavailable is not zero, favorable, safe, or not applicable.

## Q09. Why does the challenger not replace the transparent model?

- Exact answer: 8 of 11 replacement rules pass; R03, R04, and R08 fail with exact AUC and subgroup degradation results.
- Evidence: `technical-appendix.md` and `evidence/checkpoint2/candidate/module-06/model-comparison.md`.
- Decision consequence: retain the transparent model.
- Limit: retention does not establish clinical validity or permit real-patient scoring.

## Q10. Who may benefit, carry burden, be excluded, or face harm?

- Exact answer: patients may gain a testing opportunity or face unnecessary testing, missed testing, cost, delay, confusion, privacy, or access harm; clinicians and staff may carry interruption, repeated work, cognitive load, and hidden follow-up work.
- Evidence: `workflow-patient-consequences.md` and `accessible-communication.md`.

- Limit: these are consequences to examine, not observed local outcomes.

- Limit: the current record does not claim observed staff or patient views.
