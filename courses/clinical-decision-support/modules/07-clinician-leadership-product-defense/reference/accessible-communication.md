# Accessible communication record

The teaching package is complete, but the clinical idea needs revision before anyone asks to test it with local patient data. No score cutoff has been approved. One accessibility failure and one silent system failure remain in the frozen evidence. The more complex model also failed three required comparison rules, so the transparent model stays in place. The current decision is to revise, assign local owners, and return with an accessible and independently reviewed proposal.

## Frontline message

No clinical system is changing. No patient is being scored. No card will appear. The current materials are fictional teaching evidence. A future local team would need staff input on interruption, hidden follow-up work, overrides, missing information, patient questions, language and disability access, and how to report a failure without blame. Staff must know who receives concerns and who can pause or stop the work.

## Patient-facing message for a future co-design session

The idea would ask a clinician to consider whether a blood sugar test needs confirmation. It would not diagnose diabetes or place an order. Before any local evaluation, patients and caregivers would need to review what information is used, what the message means, what happens when information is missing, how to correct context, how language and disability access work, what extra testing or cost could occur, and how to raise a privacy or safety concern.

## Equivalent access checks

- Every chart or visual fact has an exact table and structured text alternative.
- Status does not rely on color.
- Headings and table headers preserve reading order.
- Technical terms are defined where they appear.
- The full recommendation, failed evidence, uncertainty, owners, and prohibited actions remain available in text.
- Written, supported oral, recorded, or live defense routes use the same 14 questions and evidence standard.

The malformed-card fixture remains blocked. This record does not claim that the frozen prototype is accessible.
