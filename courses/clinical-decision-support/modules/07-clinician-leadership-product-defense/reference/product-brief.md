# Clinical decision support product brief

## Decision

The fictional `CGH-GIM-01` governance council must decide whether the current teaching concept is ready to support a request for local approval of a bounded silent-mode evaluation. The reference recommendation is `revise before seeking local silent-mode approval`.

## Intended use

- Primary user: clinician responsible for the current adult encounter.
- Workflow moment: after required encounter information is available and before the encounter closes.
- Intended support: a nonbinding advisory asking the clinician to consider whether confirmatory HbA1c testing is appropriate.
- Human action: review the current record and clinical context, then consider testing, defer, dismiss, or take no action.
- Prohibited action: diagnosis, automatic order, blocked workflow, treatment change, denial, nonclinical targeting, or action without clinician review.
- Patient consequences requiring review: unnecessary testing, missed testing opportunity, confusion, access burden, privacy concern, and inequitable exclusion.
- Clinician consequences requiring review: interruption, repeated work, cognitive load, loss of trust, and hidden follow-up work.

## Current product state

`panel-t003` is a passive contextual panel fixture for mechanics testing. `0.03000000` is an unaccepted sandbox fixture. No clinical threshold is accepted. The FHIR R4 and CDS Hooks resources are teaching shapes, not conformance artifacts. The local sandbox is nonnetworked and has no clinical action route.

## Why revision is required

The frozen candidate contains a blocked malformed-card accessibility defect, a detected silent failure, 17 inherited failure modes, no accepted clinical threshold, no local validity or utility evidence, and three failed ML replacement rules. A curriculum package can be complete while the CDS proposal remains unready for a local approval request.

## Owners

The APP-4 faculty owner owns curriculum progression. The fictional governance council owns the teaching disposition. Local clinical, patient-safety, workflow, access, interoperability, privacy, security, model-risk, and operational owners would have to own any future real evaluation. No learner, analyst, model, or agent owns a clinical decision.
