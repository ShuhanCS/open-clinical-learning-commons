# Leadership reflection

The difficult decision is not whether the package is technically detailed. It is whether the evidence supports the next action. Here it does not yet support a request for local silent-mode approval.

The strongest part of the record is its chain of custody and explicit failure handling. The largest practical limit is the gap between historical or synthetic teaching evidence and a locally owned clinical purpose, threshold, workflow, accessibility, and safety protocol.

Leadership means keeping those two judgments separate. The curriculum package can pass because it is complete, reproducible, and bounded. The CDS recommendation can still be revise. A favorable model metric, full score, or passing mechanics test does not remove the blocked defect, silent-failure risk, patient consequences, staff work, or absent local authority.

The learner owns the clarity and traceability of the recommendation. Human clinical and governance owners retain decisions about purpose, threshold, evaluation, stop, restart, and retirement. An agent may assist with drafting or checking but cannot own evidence, grades, clinical meaning, or approval.
