# Clinician-led session plan

## Session 1: Clinical evidence and uncertainty conference, 4 hours

Joe Joseph, MD, or the approved clinician lead asks the learner to explain the intended use, evidence, calibration, threshold status, burden, failures, safety case, monitoring plan, and ML decision as one bounded clinical decision support case. The learner must identify the strongest evidence, the largest uncertainty, and every absent authority.

## Session 2: Patient, workflow, and staff voice lab, 4 hours

The clinician lead tests patient access, language and disability access, unnecessary and missed testing consequences, clinician interruption, hidden follow-up work, automation bias, clinical override, staff voice, blame-free reporting, and stop rights. The learner names who may benefit, carry burden, be excluded, or face harm.

## Session 3: Product recommendation and stewardship studio, 4 hours

The clinician lead reviews the product brief, alternatives, revision path, future evaluation prerequisites, stewardship owners, monitoring, silent-failure detection, escalation, fallback, restart, retirement, and communication. The reference recommendation remains `revise before seeking local silent-mode approval`.

## Session 4: Governance council defense, 4 hours

The clinician lead chairs a 14-question defense with clinical, patient-safety, workflow, access, interoperability, model-risk, privacy, security, and governance challenges. The panel records conditions, disagreement, package status, CDS recommendation, and final-checkpoint permission separately.

If the designated clinician is unavailable, the program may use an approved recorded case conference plus a qualified clinician-led synchronous defense that preserves the same evidence, questions, challenge standard, and feedback.
