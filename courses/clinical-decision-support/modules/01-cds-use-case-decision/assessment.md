## Progression decisions

- `continue`: every gate passes and no material condition remains.
- `continue with conditions`: every gate passes and named human reviews or bounded evidence decisions remain open.
- `revise`: one or more required records or boundaries need repair before Module 02.
- `refer`: the intended use, clinical question, governance, privacy, or evidence problem requires a different owner.

The reference decision is `continue with conditions` for curriculum construction. It is not permission to fit a model, score a patient, choose a threshold, fire an alert, or connect to a clinical system.
