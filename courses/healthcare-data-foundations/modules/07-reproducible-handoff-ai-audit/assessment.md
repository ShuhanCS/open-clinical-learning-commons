# Assessment: Reproducible toolkit candidate and technical handoff

## Course value

Module 07 creates the candidate for the source curriculum's 35-point final component. The separate final checkpoint verifies this exact candidate, conducts the defense, and records the final disposition. No additional module weight is added.

## Submission

Submit:

- the complete 90-file toolkit candidate;
- complete validator output;
- full repository commit hash;
- annotated tag `fnd1-handoff-v0.1.0` when review authorizes it;
- reproduction environment identity;
- material AI-audit evidence; and
- availability for the technical handoff defense.

Do not submit the source ZIP, generated SQLite database, virtual environment, caches, secrets, credentials, workplace data, or patient data.

## Required preparation

1. Start from an accepted or conditionally accepted Checkpoint 2 version 0.1.0 package.
2. Assemble into a new target with `assemble_toolkit.py`.
3. Inspect all 74 immutable manifest rows.
4. Complete every learner-owned release record.
5. Verify the source, repository, commit, version, and proposed tag.
6. Write the data brief, limitations, change log, and release notes.
7. Record the exact clean-reproduction route and comparisons.
8. Log every material AI use and independently audit one material step.
9. Complete the score, checklist, handoff brief, and ten responses.
10. Validate without `--starter`, commit the exact candidate, and complete the defense.

## Noncompensable gates

- accepted Checkpoint 2 version and progression disposition;
- exact 90-file tree and 74-row immutable manifest;
- accepted analytic grain and fingerprint;
- D01 through D20 and N01 through N08 preserved;
- exact descriptive denominators, intervals, and claim limits;
- exact F01 through F03 access routes;
- all 23 pipeline-source files fingerprinted;
- explicit source, rights, synthetic status, environment, and reproduction route;
- no hidden dependency or manually edited output;
- no source ZIP, database, secret, restricted data, or local absolute path;
- complete change log, release notes, data brief, limitations, checklist, and validation;
- complete prompt log and one independently verified material AI audit;
- no AI output treated as evidence by itself;
- no unsupported real-world, trend, forecast, process-control, effect, or causal claim;
- adequate technical defense; and
- allowed disposition.

## Defense

Give an eight-minute handoff and answer questions for approximately seven minutes. Cover source and use, schema and grain, cohort and denominator, analytic construction, quality, descriptive evidence, accessibility, reproduction, AI verification, and the recommended disposition.

## Progression rule

Only `accept` and `accept with conditions` permit final-checkpoint review. A condition must name an owner, due point, verification, and escalation trigger; it cannot waive a failed gate.
