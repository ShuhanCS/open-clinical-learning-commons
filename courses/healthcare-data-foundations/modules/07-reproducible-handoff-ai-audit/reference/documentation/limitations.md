# Limitations and conditions

- The source is synthetic and older; it is not evidence about current or real patients, organizations, populations, care, cost, or outcomes.
- The analytic table is a selected 374-person cohort, not the full source population or hospital volume.
- Source observation cannot prove care did not occur elsewhere.
- Structural missingness remains blank and is not zero.
- Five accepted ages at least 100 and high utilization counts remain supported extremes rather than automatic errors.
- Small race, urgent-state, and synthetic-death rows remain exact internal teaching results and require cautious display.
- Cross-tabs, rates, and index-class strata are descriptive and unadjusted.
- Wilson intervals describe arithmetic uncertainty for the fixed synthetic teaching cohort only.
- The 20-quarter sequence is selected-index chronology, not a tested trend, forecast, process-control result, or causal pattern.
- Equivalent access is technically recorded; named human accessibility review remains pending.
- Windows reproduction is recorded; named macOS and Linux reproduction remains pending.
- Named human review across faculty, technical, clinical, privacy, AI, and independent reproduction remains pending.
- A changed immutable artifact, altered denominator, hidden condition, broken access route, restricted-data exposure, or unsupported claim requires revision or referral.
