# FND-2 handoff acceptance

## Accepted technical foundation

FND-2 receives the 90-file FND-1 Module 07 version 0.1.0 candidate and its final checkpoint 0.1.0 review records from https://github.com/ShuhanCS/open-clinical-learning-commons. The accepted learner handoff must identify the reviewed 100-file commit and annotated tag `fnd1-toolkit-v0.1.0`.

## Permitted use

The toolkit may support public technical education and downstream method development on the synthetic cohort while every recorded condition, denominator, access route, and claim limit remains visible.

## Conditions

C01 requires named human decisions and the real learner defense before alpha. C02 requires named macOS and Linux reproduction before stable. C03 requires the final learner commit and annotated tag at acceptance. The owners, evidence, verifiers, and escalation triggers are in `reviewer-record.md` and `final-disposition.md`.

## Prohibited claims and uses

The handoff does not authorize production deployment, clinical approval, real-patient use, or real clinical, operational, performance, safety, access, equity, utilization, cost, population, trend, forecast, process-control, effect, or causal claims.

## Support and change notification

The FND-1 faculty owner is the support owner. FND-2 must be notified before a source, code, data, quality, denominator, figure, access, AI, version, or condition change. Changed candidate evidence returns to Module 07 and a new version decision.

## Stop or referral triggers

Stop use and revise or refer for a changed hash, ambiguous grain or denominator, restricted data, hidden dependency, failed access route, incomplete AI audit, failed gate, inadequate defense, unsupported claim, or unresolved condition beyond its due point.

## Receiver acknowledgment

The FND-2 receiver remains unassigned under C01. Progression is allowed with conditions for this technical reference; the receiver must record name, date, decision, and acknowledgment for a learner release.
