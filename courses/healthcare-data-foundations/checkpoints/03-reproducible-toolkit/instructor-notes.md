# Instructor notes: Reproducible healthcare data toolkit

## Purpose

Decide whether another instructor or analyst can receive the complete FND-1 technical foundation, reproduce it, understand its limits, and know when to stop. Review the accepted evidence; do not ask the learner to redesign or recalculate it during the checkpoint.

## Before the review

1. Confirm the published last day of the assigned half-term in the official calendar.
2. Assemble the instructor reference and run complete validation.
3. Confirm that the learner candidate passed Module 07 complete validation before final assembly.
4. Compare the learner's 90-row candidate manifest to the reviewed commit.
5. Scan for archives, databases, credentials, personal paths, restricted data, and undeclared dependencies.
6. Arrange an equivalent defense route when needed without lowering the technical standard.
7. Assign the health-system analytics engineering lead and every required review role.

## Review sequence

Review in this order:

1. repository, commit, version, candidate manifest, and proposed tag;
2. source rights, schema, grain, cohort, and time zero;
3. D01 through D20, N01 through N08, and stop rules;
4. denominators, intervals, small results, and interpretation limits;
5. F01 through F03 tables, exports, alternatives, and non-color cues;
6. clean reproduction, exact comparisons, and hidden dependencies;
7. AI inventory, prompt log, material audit, independent evidence, and human ownership;
8. handoff, ten responses, reviewer record, conditions, and disposition.

The released Module 07 response record is the technical answer key. Accept different wording when the learner preserves the exact evidence and limits.

## Scoring and defense

Score the eight criteria once in `final-score.csv`. Record the five H01 components in `defense-score.csv`; those five rows must total the H01 score. Do not compensate for a failed gate with points.

The defense should take about 15 minutes: eight minutes for the handoff and seven minutes for questions. The learner may use the toolkit, but should explain it rather than read prepared text. An equivalent written or recorded route is allowed.

## Conditions

Every condition needs an ID, owner, due point, evidence, verifier, closure status, and escalation trigger. Human review and cross-platform maturity may remain explicit conditions in a technical reference. A real learner release still needs the required reviewers, defense, exact reviewed commit, and annotated tag.

Return the work without scoring when a candidate file changes, a prohibited file appears, the grain or denominator is unclear, reproduction requires manual edits, an access route fails, the AI audit cannot be checked, an unsupported real-world claim appears, or the defense is inadequate.

## Reference fixture

The reference assembles 100 files and a 90-row, 11,804-byte candidate manifest with SHA-256 `200df43e17926e29cc09aa89427a04205fd39ac289aebdf1217f952b188b89a0`. Complete reference validation passes 493 checks. Starter validation passes 404 checks.

The reference disposition is `accept with conditions`. Its open conditions cover named human decisions and a real learner defense, named macOS and Linux reproduction, and the final reviewed commit and annotated tag. The fixture is not evidence that those human actions have occurred.
