# Assessment: Quality, descriptive, and accessible evidence release

## Course value

Checkpoint 2 is worth 25 course points. Module 04 evidence owns 13.75 points, Module 05 evidence owns 6.25 points, and Module 06 evidence owns 5.00 points. These are shares of one checkpoint, not three added course weights.

## Submission

Submit:

- the complete assembled checkpoint folder;
- validator output;
- full Git commit hash;
- annotated tag `fnd1-checkpoint2-v0.1.0`; and
- availability for the technical handoff defense.

Do not edit the 35 copied artifacts. A needed change returns to the owning module and requires a new version decision.

## Required preparation

1. Start from accepted or conditionally accepted Module 04, 05, and 06 packages.
2. Assemble into a new target with `assemble_checkpoint.py`.
3. Inspect `artifact-contract.csv`, `release-manifest.csv`, and `checkpoint-summary.csv`.
4. Complete every `REPLACE` prompt with evidence-backed content.
5. Reconcile D01 through D20, N01 through N08, all descriptive result families, and F01 through F03.
6. Perform and record the accessibility checks you actually completed.
7. Validate without `--starter`.
8. Commit the exact reviewed state and complete the defense.
9. Tag only after `accept` or `accept with conditions`.

## Noncompensable gates

- accepted Module 04, 05, and 06 IDs, versions, and decisions;
- exact 35-row artifact contract and release manifest;
- accepted 374-row, 29-field analytic table fingerprint;
- 28 passing quality rules, D01 through D20 resolved, and N01 through N08 retained;
- complete descriptive evidence with exact denominators and interval meaning;
- three exact tables, three PNG files, three SVG files, and three structured alternatives;
- no color-only meaning, fabricated access test, invented uncertainty, or changed figure value;
- no structural missingness converted to zero;
- no real-population, volume, trend, forecast, process-control, or causal claim;
- complete source, transformation, reproduction, accessibility, and AI records;
- protected-target and complete validation passes;
- adequate learner defense; and
- explicit progression disposition.

## Defense questions

1. Why does the accepted table remain one row per selected person?
2. Which defects were corrected, and which unusual values remain conditions?
3. Why is the quality decision `proceed with conditions`?
4. Which missing values are structural rather than zero?
5. What exact denominator and window support one rate?
6. Why can RT01 not be added to RT02 through RT04?
7. What does the F02 Wilson interval mean here?
8. How can a reviewer recover each figure without relying on color or sight?
9. Why is F03 not hospital volume or a tested trend?
10. What material AI contribution did you independently verify?
11. What must Module 07 preserve?
12. What evidence would force revision or referral?

## Progression rule

Only `accept` and `accept with conditions` permit Module 07. A condition must name an owner, due point, verification, and escalation trigger; it cannot waive a failed gate.
