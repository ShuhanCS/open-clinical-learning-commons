# Accessibility review

The DAG is available as editable Mermaid, node CSV, edge CSV, narrative, and SVG. A learner does not need to perceive position, color, or arrow geometry to recover the complete structure.

Every model result and decision is available as text or CSV. No score depends on a screenshot. Table headings state the unit, method, quantity, and boundary. The SVG uses high-contrast dark text on a light background and includes a descriptive title and long description.

Keyboard-only and screen-reader users can follow the numbered workflow in `README.md`. If Mermaid rendering is unavailable, `dag-narrative.md`, `dag-nodes.csv`, and `dag-edges.csv` are the authoritative equivalent route.
