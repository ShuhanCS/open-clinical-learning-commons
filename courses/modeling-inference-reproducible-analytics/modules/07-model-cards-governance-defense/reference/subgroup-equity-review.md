# Reference subgroup and equity review

The exact test evidence contains gender, race, ethnicity, and index-class group rows. Gender rows have 33 and 42 people with two outcomes each. White and non-Hispanic rows contain all four outcomes; emergency has three; inpatient has one. Asian, Black, Native, Hispanic, and inpatient rows are suppressed, so five of ten rows do not report performance metrics.

Suppression protects against overreading cells with fewer than 20 rows or fewer than two outcomes/nonoutcomes. Unsuppressed rows remain descriptive and uncertain. Missing or suppressed metrics are not zero and do not show equal performance. The evidence cannot rank groups, certify fairness, establish absence of harm, support differential thresholds, or justify clinical action.

A live fairness evaluation would require a justified population, group definitions, adequate representation and outcomes, missingness assessment, uncertainty, consequence analysis, patient and stakeholder input, prospective monitoring, and named governance. Until then the recommendation remains teaching use only. Any request for subgroup action is referred and stops use.
