# Reference rollback, stop, and retirement

## Safe fallback

The safe fallback is no model or forecast action. Continue only ordinary teaching with static accepted evidence while the candidate is quarantined. Do not roll back to an older model merely because it is available.

## Immediate stop events

Stop for a changed source or manifest hash; prohibited or identifiable data; leakage or future data; failed accepted test; seeded failure accepted; wrong label or denominator; missing trace or owner; unsupported clinical, staffing, causal, fairness, safety, or deployment claim; material accessibility failure; or evidence outside the declared population and purpose.

## Notification and investigation

The model-risk lead opens the record, preserves evidence, notifies faculty and the affected specialist owner, states scope and fallback, investigates root cause, records correction or referral, and reruns all gates. No silent repair is allowed.

## Rollback rule

Rollback is permitted only to a previously accepted and still applicable version with verified source, use, evidence, and governance. The public reference has no deployed predecessor, so its rollback is the safe fallback of no model or forecast action.

## Retirement

Retire when the purpose ends, source is unavailable, outcome meaning changes, evidence cannot be refreshed, conditions remain unresolved, a safer method replaces the candidate, or governance withdraws support. Preserve the release, rationale, last permitted use, notification, and archive; revoke active use and proposed tags that do not match an accepted commit.
