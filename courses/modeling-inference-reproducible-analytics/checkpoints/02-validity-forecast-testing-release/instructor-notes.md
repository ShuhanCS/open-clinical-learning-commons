# Instructor notes

## Position

Checkpoint 2 is a cumulative defense, not a new analysis assignment. Ask learners to show how a claim changes when causal structure, missingness, dependence, temporal validation, failure testing, and agent accountability are considered together.

## Review order

1. Validate immutable evidence and source fingerprints.
2. Read the cumulative interpretation for claim boundaries.
3. Score V01 through H01.
4. adjudicate all 25 gates.
5. review the conditions register and human sign-off.
6. conduct the 12-question defense.
7. record disposition and Module 07 permission.

## High-value probes

- Ask why a prediction feature set is not automatically a causal adjustment set.
- Ask what 263 structural blanks mean and why they are not zero.
- Ask why the CDC series cannot support a single-hospital staffing decision.
- Ask the learner to identify the fold where last-value beats damped Holt.
- Ask which agent claim was rejected and which was independently retained.
- Ask what exact event sends the work back to Module 04, 05, or 06.
