# Clinician and patient leadership session plan

## Segment 1: Evidence and clinical meaning conference, 4 hours

- Review the accepted Week 3 and Week 6 decisions.
- Separate measurement, response, linked, comment, group, partnership, and model evidence.
- Identify what the public-derived and synthetic evidence cannot establish.
- Define the service decision, authority, safety boundary, and evidence synthesis.

Joe Joseph, MD, SFHM, is the designated clinician for this segment, subject to direct participation confirmation.

## Segment 2: Patient partnership and shared-authority lab, 4 hours

- Review whose voice is present, generated, absent, or still required.
- Test the patient-facing summary for clarity, burden, access, privacy, and unsupported claims.
- Record partner decision rights, disagreements, requested changes, and team responses.
- Assign shared clinical, patient, operational, analytic, access, and governance roles.

The named patient/caregiver co-lead and Dr. Joseph co-lead this segment. Construction may test the records while the co-lead remains pending, but alpha delivery may not claim patient co-leadership without direct participation.

## Segment 3: Recommendation, workflow, and accountability studio, 4 hours

- Choose one allowed recommendation.
- Draft a universal-offer prospective measurement and improvement protocol without model targeting.
- Review workflow, capacity, language, format, proxy, interpreter, no-contact, safety, and data needs.
- Define measures, feedback, monitoring, ownership, stop, escalation, and restart rules.

The clinician, patient/caregiver co-lead, improvement lead, and operations owner share the review.

## Segment 4: Leadership council defense, 4 hours

- Deliver the accessible clinical and patient-facing handoff.
- Answer all 14 defense questions.
- Respond to clinical, patient-impact, operations, methods, equity, access, privacy, and governance challenges.
- Record conditions, recommendation, package status, and final-checkpoint permission separately.

The clinician and patient/caregiver co-lead co-chair the defense. Faculty record the course score and technical conditions.

## Delivery boundary

If Dr. Joseph or the patient/caregiver co-lead cannot participate, the program must pause the named alpha delivery or approve and document a changed instructor contract. A recording may be used only with permission and an equivalent live discussion and defense. The same evidence, questions, accessibility routes, and assessment standard apply.
