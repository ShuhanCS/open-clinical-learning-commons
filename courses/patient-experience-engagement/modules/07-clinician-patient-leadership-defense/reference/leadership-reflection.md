# Leadership reflection

The strongest decision is not the fastest launch. It is a shared recommendation that keeps the accepted evidence intact, names what remains unknown, and gives the clinician and patient/caregiver co-lead real authority to revise or stop the work.

The package changed from a technical release into an accountable proposal in three ways. First, it translates measurement, response, linked-use, comment, group, and model evidence into one bounded organizational question. Second, it makes a universal offer and accessible choices part of the design instead of using a model to select people. Third, it treats returning results and reasons as work with an owner and a stop consequence.

The central unresolved issue is patient partnership. The reference uses simulated records and generated comments, so it cannot say what local patients or caregivers want. Direct co-lead review may change the questions, routes, burden limit, feedback interval, authority, or the recommendation itself. That possibility is a required part of the decision, not a defect to hide.

The current package therefore earns curriculum acceptance while the organizational recommendation remains `revise before testing`. Leadership here means protecting that distinction and leaving a clear route to revise, refer, or stop.
