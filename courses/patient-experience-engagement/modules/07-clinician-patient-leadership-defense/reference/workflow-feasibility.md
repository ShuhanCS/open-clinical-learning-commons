# Workflow feasibility review

- Current status: `not established locally`.
- Proposed population: `all locally eligible adult inpatient discharges under an approved rule`.
- Offer rule: `universal offer; no response model or group targeting`.
- Access routes: `phone, mail, web, interpreter-supported, proxy-supported, accessible format, and no contact`.
- Proposed steps: `confirm eligibility; record language and format; offer choices; check understanding; route questions safely; record consent and contact choice; collect feedback; review; return results`.
- Clinical safety owner: `pending named local owner`.
- Patient partnership owner: `pending named co-lead`.
- Operations and capacity owner: `pending named local owner`.
- Survey and data owners: `pending named local owners`.

The teaching package does not contain local discharge volume, staffing, time, interpreter and accessibility capacity, contact infrastructure, question-routing capacity, privacy process, or review schedule. It cannot establish feasibility.

Before any authorization, the local team must walk through ordinary, after-hours, language-support, proxy, disability-access, declined-contact, unresolved-question, and safety-escalation paths. It must record staffing, time, failure routes, ownership, and patient/caregiver review. An unavailable requested route stops or narrows the proposal.
