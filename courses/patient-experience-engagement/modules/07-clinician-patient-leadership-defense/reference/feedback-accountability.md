# Feedback and accountability

After each review cycle, the improvement owner prepares an aggregate summary with eligible opportunities, offers, choices, responses, item missingness, supported group evidence, suppressed cells, access failures, burden, unwanted contact, safety routing, staff time, open disagreements, and missed return dates.

The named patient/caregiver co-lead and patient advisory group review the summary in accessible formats. The team records each requested change, owner, due date, disposition, and reason. A disagreement remains visible unless the person who raised it agrees that it is resolved.

Within the directly agreed interval, patients receive a plain-language response through requested print, phone, web, interpreter-supported, proxy-supported, and accessible formats. The response states what changed, what did not change, why, who owns the next step, and what remains open. It never publishes generated comments as patient voice or exposes unsupported groups.

Failure to return results by the agreed date is an accountability failure. Repeated failure stops progression until ownership and capacity are repaired and the patient/caregiver co-lead agrees to restart.
