# Evidence synthesis

## Decision

The accepted evidence supports revising a bounded, universal-offer discharge-information and feedback proposal for shared clinician and patient review. It does not support fielding, contact, expected benefit, official HCAHPS reporting, group action, clinical implementation, or model deployment.

## Measurement and response

The teaching case retains the accepted HCAHPS Q22 and Q23 discharge-information pair under its exact rights, scoring, direction, skip, burden, language, access, and official-reporting limits. The target has 1,255 public-derived MEPS records. The procedural response layer has 782 synthetic respondents and 473 synthetic nonrespondents. Total nonresponse, Q22 and Q23 item missingness, base weights, transparent teaching factors, and residual bias remain separate.

## Linked evidence

Module 04 links 28,455 target events across inpatient, emergency, outpatient, and office-based settings. All source events reconcile to the accepted person source. The package preserves aligned denominators and the 12 inpatient stays that began in 2023 and continued into the 2024 event file. Telehealth is a service channel, not portal preference or engagement. The source has no portal-preference measure, and the provider-language result has only 45 valid target records and limited support.

## Patient voice and equity questions

All 420 comments are generated curriculum examples across eight themes. They are not patient testimony or prevalence evidence. The 120-record simulated coding benchmark has 80.00000000 percent agreement and Cohen's kappa 0.77142857. The fixed phrase rule has 0.78333333 benchmark accuracy and does not remove human review.

35 of 52 group estimates and 19 of 36 contrasts meet the support rule. Unsupported values remain blank without merging source categories. A supported contrast keeps a question open but does not prove inequity, discrimination, preference, cause, fairness, or a group trait.

## Partnership, improvement, and model decision

The Module 06 partnership record is simulated and contains no actual patient or caregiver statement. A named and compensated co-lead must review the questions, language, burden, access routes, disagreement, and proposed authority before alpha.

The proposal uses a universal offer with phone, mail, web, interpreter-supported, proxy-supported, accessible-format, and no-contact choices. Fourteen prospective measures cover implementation, process, response, outcome, access, balancing, accountability, and safety.

The transparent and bounded-ML teaching-composite absolute biases are 2.48289986 and 2.39922466 percentage points. The 0.08367520-point improvement is below the prespecified 0.50 threshold. The random forest does not change the response-adjustment decision, and the transparent benchmark remains.

## Leadership conclusion

The package is ready for final curriculum construction with conditions. The organizational proposal is not ready for testing. It needs actual patient partnership, local eligibility and workflow evidence, capacity review, approved prospective collection, confirmed access routes, privacy and governance approval, and shared final review.
