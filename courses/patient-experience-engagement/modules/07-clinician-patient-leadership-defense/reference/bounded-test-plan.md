# Bounded prospective measurement and improvement plan

- Status: `design proposal; not authorized`.
- Setting: `one locally approved adult inpatient service or unit`.
- Eligibility: `locally approved adult inpatient discharge rule with exclusions recorded`.
- Reach: `universal offer to every eligible discharge`.
- Targeting model: `none`.
- Run-in: `two weeks for workflow, access, safety, and data-quality checks`.
- Initial test duration: `four weeks after an accepted run-in`.
- Comparison: `approved baseline or phased comparison defined before collection`.
- Review cadence: `weekly during run-in and test; immediate for safety, privacy, consent, or access failure`.
- Final authority: `named clinical decision owner and patient/caregiver co-lead under local governance`.

## Learning aim

Learn whether the service can offer accessible discharge-information and feedback choices, route questions safely, collect complete response and burden evidence, and return results without unacceptable burden, unwanted contact, exclusion, or workflow failure.

## Measurement

Retain accepted Q22 and Q23 wording and scoring for the teaching contract. Any local clarity, burden, acceptability, or workflow question remains a separate proposed item and cannot be called validated without evidence. Record eligible counts, offers, choices, responses, item missingness, access failures, unwanted contact, safety routing, staff time, and returned-results dates.

## Data and analysis boundary

Use only a locally approved prospective data contract. Prespecify denominators, missingness, support, suppression, comparison, and review rules. Use the transparent response adjustment as the teaching benchmark. Do not train on comment text, target patients or groups, or issue official HCAHPS results.

## Authorization boundary

The plan requires direct patient/caregiver co-lead review, clinical approval, operations and access readiness, privacy and governance approval, measurement and survey review, and a documented stop-and-restart decision before use.
