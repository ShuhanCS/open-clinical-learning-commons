# Accessibility and language review

- Review status: `pass with conditions for curriculum construction`.
- Patient/caregiver co-lead review: `pending before alpha`.
- Plain-language summary: `present`.
- Structured tables: `CSV records present with descriptive headers`.
- Digital route: `keyboard-readable text and tables`.
- Print route: `plain-text and table equivalent`.
- Phone route: `scripted read-aloud equivalent required locally`.
- Caption and transcript route: `required for recorded or live media`.
- Interpreter support: `required for requested languages`.
- Proxy support: `allowed by approved local choice and consent rules`.
- Accessible formats: `large print, screen-reader compatible, and other directly requested formats`.
- Defense alternative: `synchronous or asynchronous route using the same 14 questions and evidence`.
- Generated comments as patient voice: `prohibited`.
- Unsupported group claims: `prohibited`.

Accessibility is not established by listing routes. A named access and language owner and patient/caregiver co-lead must test the locally offered routes, record failures, and pause any route that cannot provide equivalent participation.
