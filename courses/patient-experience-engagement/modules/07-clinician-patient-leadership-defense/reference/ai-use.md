# Agent-use record

- Material agent use: `draft structure, evidence retrieval from accepted packages, consistency checks, and validation code`.
- Patient or restricted data shared: `none`.
- Generated patient testimony: `none`.
- Clinical recommendation authority delegated: `none`.
- Patient-partnership authority delegated: `none`.
- Evidence paths checked: `evidence-index.csv and both accepted checkpoint manifests`.
- Different check: `nested validators, deterministic assembly, exact-value checks, and human review roles`.
- Correction record: `source records are revised in version control; accepted checkpoint evidence is never edited in place`.
- Named human owner: `APP-2 faculty owner`.
- Independent reviewer: `pending before alpha`.
- Final clinician sign-off: `pending direct confirmation and review`.
- Final patient/caregiver co-lead sign-off: `pending direct participation and review`.

An agent may suggest language or checks. It cannot invent patient voice, settle disagreement, infer local preference, authorize contact, approve the protocol, or count as independent verification.
