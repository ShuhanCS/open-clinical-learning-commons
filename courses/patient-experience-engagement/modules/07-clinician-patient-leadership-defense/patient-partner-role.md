# Patient or caregiver co-lead role

The patient or caregiver co-lead shares authority over patient-facing language, burden, access, partnership evidence, disagreements, feedback, accountability, and the leadership recommendation. This role is required for alpha teaching.

Before participation, the APP-2 faculty owner must record the co-lead's name, compensation, preparation, schedule, language and format needs, disability access, technology needs, decision rights, attribution choice, recording consent, withdrawal route, disagreement rights, and final-review terms.

The co-lead may add questions, correct language, retain disagreement, reject generated text presented as patient voice, request a narrower proposal, or withhold partnership approval. A clinician, staff member, simulated record, generated comment, or agent cannot substitute for this role.

The construction reference contains no actual patient or caregiver statement. Until a named person participates and reviews the package, the module must say `patient/caregiver co-lead pending before alpha`.
