# Instructor notes

Begin with the decision, not a survey vendor or score. Ask who needs to act, which patient-reported construct matters, who must help define it, and what evidence would change the decision.

The full CMS source is large enough to expose reporting structure and missing public values. It is not patient-level data. Do not let learners infer local response bias, subgroup experience, language access, or patient preference from hospital-level rows.

The four discharge anchors are `H_COMP_6_Y_P`, `H_COMP_6_N_P`, `H_DISCH_HELP_Y_P`, and `H_SYMPTOMS_Y_P`. Their role is to show how a broad recovery-at-home concern becomes measurable concepts. Module 02 owns instrument choice, version, rights, scoring, reliability, validity, and meaningful-change interpretation.

The reference should progress with conditions because the construct is measurable but the public source cannot answer the local decision. Patient-partner authority, target-population rules, language and proxy access, collection burden, local governance, and instrument rights remain open.
