# Patient-experience decision charter

- Decision owner: `adult inpatient patient-experience council with a patient/caregiver advisory group`
- Patient-partner authority: `co-own the construct, interpret evidence, challenge burden and access assumptions, and approve the question carried into instrument selection`
- Decision: `Is the recovery at home patient-reported construct defined well enough to enter fit-for-purpose instrument selection for a local multilingual measurement study?`
- Proposed next action: `compare candidate instruments, scoring rules, collection modes, burden, and access requirements in Module 02`
- Target population: `adults discharged alive from inpatient care who can respond directly or through an allowed proxy under the selected instrument rules`
- Care setting: `adult inpatient discharge preparation and recovery at home`
- Primary construct: `whether patients received usable information and support for recovery at home`
- Supporting constructs: `discussion of needed help, warning signs, communication clarity, access, burden, and ability to act on information`
- Measure type: `patient-reported experience measure, not a satisfaction score, patient-reported outcome, or clinical outcome`
- Public source role: `the full CMS HCAHPS hospital-level file identifies relevant reported measures and source-feasibility limits`
- Local evidence gap: `the public source is hospital level and not patient level; it does not show the local eligible frame, individual responses, subgroup experience, language, mode, proxy use, burden, or patient priorities`
- Language and access rule: `instrument selection must include multilingual availability, disability access, low-literacy use, non-digital routes, and equivalent interpretation`
- Proxy rule: `Module 02 must state whether and when proxy response is permitted and how proxy evidence is labeled`
- Burden rule: `do not add collection until patients and staff review length, timing, fatigue, duplication, and a shorter alternative`
- Evidence standard: `continue only if a fit-for-purpose instrument, valid scoring rule, accessible collection plan, target frame, and patient-partner approval can be documented`
- Claim boundary: `public CMS values describe reported hospital-level patient experience; they do not establish local need, patient-level response bias, subgroup experience, causation, or intervention benefit`
- Stop or referral trigger: `refer or revise if instrument rights, validity, language access, patient-partner authority, privacy, target-frame, or burden requirements cannot be met`
- Module 02 handoff: `one bounded recovery-at-home construct, four HCAHPS decision anchors, eight evidence needs, seven stakeholders, and unresolved instrument questions`
- Implementation status: `implementation is prohibited`
- Hospital-ranking status: `hospital ranking is prohibited`

## Decision rationale

The public source shows that recovery-at-home information is a measurable patient-experience topic. It also shows why a public hospital file cannot decide a local improvement. The next defensible step is instrument selection and local measurement design with patient/caregiver partners, not a workflow change.
