# APP-2 Module 01 assessment

Module 01 awards no separate course points. Its accepted records enter the cumulative 20-point Week 3 patient-measurement package.

Progression requires all 12 gates:

1. exact APP-2 and CMS source identity;
2. one accountable patient-experience decision;
3. a defined target population and care setting;
4. a named patient/caregiver partner role with decision rights;
5. correct experience, PREM, PROM, satisfaction, engagement, partnership, and clinical-outcome distinctions;
6. a complete patient journey and measurement-point map;
7. reproduction of all committed full-source profiles;
8. an evidence-needs record that distinguishes public evidence from local evidence still needed;
9. explicit language, disability access, proxy, mode, and burden questions;
10. no patient, hospital-ranking, causal, prevalence, or implementation overclaim;
11. complete source, AI-use, and verification records; and
12. an allowed progression decision with owned open conditions.

Allowed progression values are `continue`, `continue with conditions`, `revise`, and `refer`. Clinical action is prohibited in every Module 01 disposition.
