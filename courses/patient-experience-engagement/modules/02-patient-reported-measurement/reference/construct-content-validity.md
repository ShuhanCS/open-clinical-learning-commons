- Intended construct: `patient-reported experience of receiving information needed for recovery at home after an adult inpatient stay`
- Measure type: `PREM`
- Population and setting fit: `adult inpatient discharge; local eligibility must follow Q21 and Module 03 must test representation`
- Content represented: `help needed after discharge and written information about symptoms or health problems`
- Content omitted: `actual recovery, symptom burden, health outcome, preference, access, communication quality, and intervention benefit`
- Patient-partner judgment: `provisionally acceptable for curriculum construction; named patient/caregiver confirmation remains required before fielding`
- Validity evidence available: `official HCAHPS instrument and scoring documentation plus exact content review`
- Validity claim allowed: `the pair has content fit for the bounded discharge-information process question; this module does not establish local validity`
- Decision: `select Q22/Q23 with conditions and retain Q20 separately`
