# Measurement decision

- Decision owner: `adult inpatient patient-experience council with patient/caregiver advisory group co-ownership`
- Intended construct: `reported receipt of information that supports recovery at home after an adult inpatient stay`
- Selected instrument: `updated HCAHPS Discharge Information pair`
- Version: `QAG V19.0 instrument effective January 1, 2025; April 2026 addendum checked`
- Selected items: `Q22 help needed after discharge and Q23 written symptoms or health-problems information`
- Screener and skip logic: `Q21 another health facility makes Q22/Q23 not applicable; missing and not applicable never become no`
- Score: `mean of question-level yes proportions using question-specific answered denominators; local calculation remains unadjusted and unofficial`
- Language and mode plan: `offer an accessible route from the 9 mail, 4 phone, and 9 web official files according to preference and need`
- Rights and naming: `public-domain HCAHPS-derived items; local, unadjusted, unofficial; no official-score comparison`
- Unresolved conditions: `named patient/caregiver confirmation, local access operations, fielding burden, response representation, consent, and mode effects`
- Clinical action: `prohibited`
- Hospital ranking: `prohibited`
- Module 03 handoff: `exact item and score contract may enter response and representation study with conditions; weighting starts there`
