# Proxy, access, and burden record

- Proxy rule: `support may help with access or response entry only under the approved administration rule; never silently substitute another person's experience`
- Patient permission: `obtain and record permission for support whenever the patient can provide it`
- Low-literacy route: `offer trained read-aloud or telephone support without paraphrasing the item`
- Disability-access route: `provide screen-reader and keyboard-compatible digital access, large print, and an equivalent telephone or supported route`
- Non-digital route: `mail and telephone remain available; web access is not assumed`
- Language-preference rule: `ask and honor preference using an official version when available; a new translation requires review and cannot be labeled official automatically`
- Burden estimate: `Q21 plus Q22/Q23 is a short local item sequence, but timing and cumulative contact burden require local testing`
- Burden stop rule: `pause or refer when the patient declines, becomes distressed or fatigued, lacks an accessible route, or needs unapproved interpretation`
- Patient-partner authority: `the patient/caregiver partner may require revision or referral before fielding`
