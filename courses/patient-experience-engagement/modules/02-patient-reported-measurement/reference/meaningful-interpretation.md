# Meaningful interpretation

- Score direction: `a higher yes percentage represents more positive reported discharge-information experience`
- Established meaningful-change threshold: `none established in this module`
- What one score can describe: `the proportion of answered responses in the positive category under the stated scoring and adjustment rules`
- What one score cannot establish: `clinical recovery, satisfaction, causation, intervention benefit, local representativeness, or a meaningful change`
- Official public-score rule: `retain the CMS H_COMP_6_Y_P value because published item percentages are rounded and adjusted`
- Patient-facing interpretation: `among people whose answers entered the score, this summarizes whether they reported receiving the two kinds of discharge information; it does not show whether they recovered well`
- Decision: `use descriptive wording and do not attach a minimally important difference`
