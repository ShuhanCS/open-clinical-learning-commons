# Module 02 instructor notes

Begin with the measure-selection decision, not the calculation. The updated Q22/Q23 pair is a patient-reported experience measure about discharge information. It is not a PROM, clinical outcome, or satisfaction scale.

Have learners reproduce both teaching averages. The item-question composite is 81.17588933 percent; the person-weighted mean is 80.0 percent. The difference is expected because missingness is distributed differently by question and person.

Use the complete 160-record synthetic subset only to demonstrate a two-item calculation. Raw alpha is 0.6549707602 and standardized alpha is 0.6578064258. Neither value validates HCAHPS. The QAG reliability target of at least 0.8 concerns hospital-level signal-to-noise reliability, generally with at least 300 completed surveys over 12 months.

The public concordance has 3,610 complete facilities. A simple average of published Q22/Q23 item percentages equals the official composite for 1,734 facilities and differs for 1,876, from -3.0 to 3.5 percentage points. Learners should explain adjustment and rounding, retain the official composite, and avoid ranking.

Stop the release if a learner changes item wording, calls a partial survey official HCAHPS, sets a meaningful-change threshold without evidence, treats synthetic output as observed evidence, or moves into weighting. Module 03 owns response and representation.
