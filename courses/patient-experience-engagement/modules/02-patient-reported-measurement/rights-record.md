# Rights, naming, and comparison record

- Instrument owner: U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services.
- Instrument status: updated HCAHPS questions 1 through 32 are works of the U.S. Government and are in the public domain.
- Trademark: CAHPS is a registered trademark of the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality.
- Exact-use rule: the official name depends on preserving the required core instrument, wording, order, and administration rules.
- Modification rule: deleting, rewriting, or reordering core questions creates a non-CAHPS instrument unless authoritative guidance states otherwise.
- Teaching label: a short local use of Q22/Q23 is `public-domain HCAHPS-derived items; local, unadjusted, unofficial`.
- Comparison rule: a partial or modified local item set may not be compared with official adjusted HCAHPS scores.
- Translation rule: only the retained official language versions are represented as official. A local translation requires separate review, naming, and validation.
- Endorsement: CMS, AHRQ, and the HCAHPS Project Team do not endorse this teaching package.
