# Assessment

The response and linked-evidence analysis is worth 25 course points. It enters the Week 6 checkpoint once.

| Component | Points | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Source identity and governed linkage | 5 | all 25 official sources, accepted Week 3 identity, person-event grain, linkage key, released-ID rule, and related-event handling |
| Reconciliation and denominators | 5 | exact person-to-event reconciliation, carry-in period rule, denominator registry, missing and inapplicable values, and person-versus-event distinction |
| Access, communication, and digital-service evidence | 5 | ten prespecified measures, survey design, uncertainty, limited-support flag, telehealth interpretation, and explicit portal evidence gap |
| Service use and linked pattern analysis | 5 | four service settings, aligned support, uncertainty, synthetic/public separation, and a noncausal linked interpretation |
| Reproduction, claims, and defense | 5 | clean rebuild, 20 gates, complete AI record, responsible claims, progression decision, and oral defense |
| Total | 25 |  |

All 20 gates are noncompensable. A numeric score cannot override a changed source, broken link, hidden mismatch, wrong denominator, direct source identifier in a released table, missing survey design, unsupported portal or preference claim, synthetic data presented as observed patient evidence, causal statement, or absent human accountability.

The reference earns 25 of 25 points and records `continue with conditions`. That curriculum-construction result does not authorize a local analysis or workflow change.
