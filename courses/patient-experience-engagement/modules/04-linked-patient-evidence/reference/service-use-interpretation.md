# Service-use interpretation

Person totals reconcile exactly to 28,455 linked event rows: 1,692 inpatient stays, 1,601 emergency visits, 4,651 outpatient visits, and 20,511 office-based visits. All 1,255 people have at least one inpatient event. The base-weighted estimates show any emergency use for 70.89748576 percent, any outpatient use for 50.40502704 percent, and any office-based use for 92.74535872 percent of the target.

The 855 reciprocal emergency-inpatient pairs remain related events. They are not treated as unrelated encounters. Twelve inpatient stays begin in 2023 and continue into the 2024 annual event file; the analysis retains them under the documented source rule.

The results are descriptive service-use patterns for a public-use adult inpatient target. The interpretation is noncausal. The results do not show that communication caused service use, that one channel is better, or that a provider delivered higher-quality care.
