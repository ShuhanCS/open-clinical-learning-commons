# Digital-service interpretation

The target has 25,162 linked outpatient and office-based events. Of these, 1,813 are marked telehealth, or 7.20530959 percent unweighted and 7.37866394 percent with the event-row person weights. Among telehealth events, 629 are phone, 1,144 are video, and 38 use another mode.

This is a service-channel distribution. The accepted source suite has no portal-preference measure and no evidence that patients preferred, could access, or were engaged by a digital channel. Telehealth use must not be relabeled as portal access, patient preference, engagement, or intervention effect.
