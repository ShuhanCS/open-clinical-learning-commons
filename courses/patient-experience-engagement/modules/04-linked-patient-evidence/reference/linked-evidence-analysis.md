# Linked evidence analysis

The linked teaching analysis includes 538 synthetic respondents with Q21 home and both Q22 and Q23 answered. It compares two synthetic discharge-item groups using the accepted response-adjusted weight and links those procedural groups to public MEPS access and service-use fields.

Among 309 people with both synthetic discharge items answered yes, the weighted delayed-care-for-cost estimate is 5.15284197 percent (95 percent CI 2.98941587 to 7.31626807). Among 229 people with one or both items answered no, it is 12.69288290 percent (95 percent CI 6.93815775 to 18.44760804). The corresponding office-based means are 15.32762989 and 16.96290478 events per person. Emergency-use estimates are 70.53483613 and 70.32076801 percent.

The experience groups are synthetic and were generated from public person characteristics. The linked differences are procedural teaching associations, not observed patient-experience findings. The access supplement and annual event files also overlap in time, so the table cannot establish temporal order or causation.
