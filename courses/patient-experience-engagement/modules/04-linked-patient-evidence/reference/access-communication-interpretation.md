# Access and communication interpretation

The accepted public-use target has 1,255 adults and a base-weighted population of 18,879,474.284615. Each estimate uses its own valid-response denominator, `PERWT24F`, `VARSTR`, and `VARPSU`.

Among 1,229 valid records, 80.78856833 percent have a usual source of care (95 percent CI 78.12403635 to 83.45310031). Regular phone contact is very or somewhat difficult for 23.80306525 percent of 972 valid records. After-hours contact is very or somewhat difficult for 52.44065366 percent of 708 valid records. Providers usually or always involve the person in decisions for 74.88406042 percent of 957 valid records. Medical care was delayed because of cost for 7.61893012 percent of 1,252 valid records.

The provider-language result has only 45 valid records and is flagged `limited_support`. Its 93.59013151 percent estimate is not a broad language-access or equity conclusion. These estimates describe the public MEPS target, not a local hospital, the synthetic HCAHPS response layer, or a causal effect.
