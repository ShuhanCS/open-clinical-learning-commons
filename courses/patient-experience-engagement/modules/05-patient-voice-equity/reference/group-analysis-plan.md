# Group analysis plan

- Dimensions: `other language at home, income group, insurance coverage, and race and ethnicity`
- Reference groups: `no other language at home; middle or high income; any private insurance; non-Hispanic White only`
- Measures: `delayed care because of cost, difficult after-hours contact, usually or always involved in decisions, and any linked telehealth event`
- Weight and design: `PERWT24F with VARSTR and VARPSU; domain variance retains every sampled PSU with zero contribution outside the domain`
- Support rule: `at least 50 valid records, 10 positive records, 10 negative records, and two contributing PSUs`
- Contrast rule: `group minus fixed reference using one joint linearized survey calculation; both estimates must pass support`
- Claim boundary: `descriptive exploratory teaching evidence, not proof of inequity, cause, fairness, preference, or a group trait`
