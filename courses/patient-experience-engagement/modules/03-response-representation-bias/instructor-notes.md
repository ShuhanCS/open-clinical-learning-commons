# Module 03 instructor notes

Start with the response flow, not the weighted score. Ask learners to name the population represented by every numerator and denominator before they compare a subgroup.

The one-record missing-language cell is intentionally retained. It shows why a missing category must not be silently recoded and why a mechanically valid factor can still have weak support. The age 18 to 44, other-language, lower-income cell hits the 3.0 cap and must remain visible.

The bounded adjustment improves Q22, Q23, and the teaching composite relative to base weighting alone, but all three remain above full-frame synthetic truth. That is the central lesson. Transparent weighting can help without solving nonignorable response or item missingness.

Do not let learners call `PERWT24F` unadjusted, call the teaching factor official, treat a synthetic mode pattern as real causation, or describe a subgroup difference as inequity without a real design and adequate support.

Module 04 may begin only after all 19 response gates and the cumulative Week 3 integrity checks pass.
