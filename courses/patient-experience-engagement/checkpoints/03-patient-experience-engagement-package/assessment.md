# Final checkpoint assessment

The checkpoint records the Module 07 draft once as the final 35-point course component.

| ID | Criterion | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| M01 | Correct measurement, response, linked-evidence, and model reasoning | 7.00 |
| P01 | Patient partnership, equity, access, and shared authority | 8.00 |
| L01 | Leadership recommendation, feasibility, safety, and ownership | 8.00 |
| A01 | Patient-facing communication, monitoring, feedback, and accountability | 7.00 |
| H01 | Reproducibility, accessibility, responsible agent use, and defense | 5.00 |
| Total |  | 35.00 |

Passing requires at least 28.00 points, all 26 gates, an adequate defense, `accept` or `accept with conditions`, and one allowed organizational recommendation. Numeric strength cannot waive changed evidence, a failed gate, fabricated patient voice, inaccessible evidence, an incomplete safety route, hidden agent use, or absent ownership.

APP-2 totals 100 points: 20 at Week 3, 45 at Week 6, and 35 on the official half-term end date.
