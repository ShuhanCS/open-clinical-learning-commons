# Release acceptance and handoff

## Receiver gets

The receiver gets the exact Week 3 and Week 6 packages, the complete Module 07 candidate, the patient-facing summary, partnership and disagreement record, a bounded recommendation of `revise before testing`, workflow and access assumptions, 14 measures, 14 stop rules, a complete evidence index, and eight open conditions with owners and escalation triggers.

## Allowed use

Curriculum construction, named program review, learner instruction, and controlled assessment preparation.

## Prohibited use

Patient contact, survey fielding, official HCAHPS reporting, patient or group targeting, clinical implementation, model deployment, site or group ranking, and claims of causation, efficacy, fairness, quality, access, safety, or real-population benefit.

## Change and stop rule

Any changed candidate byte requires a new Module 07 version and renewed final review. A prohibited-data finding, failed validator, changed release identity, fabricated patient voice, inadequate defense, unsupported claim, missing owner, or unauthorized tag stops acceptance.

## Tag status

`app2-patient-experience-engagement-candidate-v0.1.0` is proposed and not created. Named human approval, direct patient review, independent reproduction, learner defense, and exact-commit verification are required before authorization.
