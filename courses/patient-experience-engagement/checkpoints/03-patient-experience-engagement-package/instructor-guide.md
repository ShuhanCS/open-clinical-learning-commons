# Final checkpoint instructor guide

- Verify the official section and half-term end date from the published calendar.
- Validate the complete candidate before reviewing its leadership judgment.
- Keep package disposition separate from organizational recommendation.
- The reference package passes because its evidence and limits are complete, not because it approves a test.
- Require exact evidence paths for material claims.
- Conduct all 14 defense questions and at least one follow-up challenge.
- Require direct patient/caregiver co-lead review before alpha.
- Record condition owners and escalation triggers.
- Do not create the proposed tag until named human approval, independent reproduction, direct patient review, and exact-commit verification are complete.
- Course acceptance does not authorize contact, fielding, official reporting, targeting, implementation, or deployment.
