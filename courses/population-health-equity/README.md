# APP-5: Data for Population Health and Equity

APP-5 is a seven-module, 112.5-hour applied course. Learners define population denominators, build and standardize rates, measure disparities, reason about place, audit targeting and fairness, design intervention monitoring, test a bounded ML challenger, and defend an accountable equity recommendation.

The continuing case is a fictional Massachusetts adult diabetes-prevention planning review built on complete, identified public tract releases and separately labeled synthetic intervention evidence. Public data may support questions and structured community review. They do not authorize real targeting, funding, outreach, eligibility, implementation, or evaluation.

## Course sequence

| Module | Title | Hours | Points |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 01 | Framing a population-health decision | 15.5 | Required gate |
| 02 | Population measures from linked data | 16.0 | 20 |
| 03 | Disparities and data limits | 16.5 | 20 |
| 04 | Place-based evidence and geographic reasoning | 16.5 | 10 |
| 05 | Targeting and fairness | 16.0 | 15 |
| 06 | Accountable intervention design, monitoring, and embedded ML | 16.0 | Required gate |
| 07 | Clinician leadership and equity recommendation | 16.0 | 35 |

Checkpoint totals are 40 points at Week 3, 25 points at Week 6, and 35 points on the official last day of the assigned half-term.
