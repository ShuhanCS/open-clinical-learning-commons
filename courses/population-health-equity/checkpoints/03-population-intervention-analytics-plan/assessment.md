# Final checkpoint assessment

The final component is scored once for 35 points.

| Criterion | Focus | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| E01 | Evidence integrity, synthesis, and exact handoff | 8.00 |
| P01 | Population-health and equity judgment | 9.00 |
| L01 | Clinician leadership and community communication | 8.00 |
| G01 | Governance, monitoring, stewardship, and accountability | 6.00 |
| H01 | Reproducibility, responsible agent use, and defense | 4.00 |
| Total |  | 35.00 |

Passing requires at least 28.00 points, all 26 gates, complete and truthful condition and reviewer records, and an adequate 16-question defense. The accepted Week 3 and Week 6 totals remain frozen at 40 and 25 points. The final score may not be duplicated.

A score cannot override a changed candidate identity, unsupported population or outcome claim, fabricated community input, hidden burden, accepted clustering challenger, missing owner, closed condition without evidence, false clinician participation, early tag, or expanded real-world authority.
