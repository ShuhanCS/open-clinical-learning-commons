# Instructor notes

## Teaching purpose

This module tests whether learners can make and defend a bounded population-health leadership recommendation from an imperfect evidence chain. The work is interpretation, accountability, communication, and governance. It is not another opportunity to change the denominator, improve a rule, hide a condition, retune the challenger, or invent an outcome.

## Review order

1. Verify both checkpoint identities and the generated release manifest.
2. Read the population intervention brief and recommendation before reviewing the score.
3. Confirm that the 40-point and 25-point checkpoints remain unscored here.
4. Check the unavailable and suppressed states, ecological limits, four rule consequences, five staff holds, 12 travel concerns, one burden concern, unavailable outcomes, and rejected clustering challenger.
5. Ask all 16 defense questions and require an accepted evidence path, practical consequence, and limit for every answer.
6. Record curriculum-package status and fictional planning recommendation separately.

## Clinician boundary

Joe Joseph, MD, SFHM, is designated under dated public sources. Do not claim that he reviewed, endorsed, or taught the package until direct confirmation exists. A qualified substitute route must preserve the same clinician-led challenge and defense standard.

## Release boundary

The reference is `accept with conditions` as a curriculum package and `recommend seeking approval for bounded structured community review` as a fictional planning recommendation. No learner may alter the frozen checkpoints, infer individual need, fabricate community agreement, close an inherited condition, accept the clustering challenger, invent an outcome, or grant contact, outreach, allocation, service, evaluation, implementation, production, or deployment authority.
