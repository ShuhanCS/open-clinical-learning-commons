# Clinician-led session plan

## Session 1: Population evidence and clinical meaning conference, 4 hours

Joe Joseph, MD, or the approved clinician lead asks the learner to explain the population, denominator, geography, period, public and synthetic source roles, modeled prevalence, uncertainty, suppression, disparity, and individual-inference limits as one bounded health decision. The learner identifies the strongest evidence, the largest uncertainty, and every absent authority.

## Session 2: Equity, access, trust, and community-accountability lab, 4 hours

The clinician lead tests who may benefit, carry travel or administrative burden, be excluded, be stigmatized, or be missing from the evidence. The learner explains language and disability access, community authority, correction, objection, disagreement, recourse, nonretaliation, pause, and stop rights without fabricating community input.

## Session 3: Intervention readiness, monitoring, and stewardship studio, 4 hours

The clinician lead reviews staffing, access, burden, the absence of outcomes, all 20 measures, six triggers, 23 incidents, evaluation limits, governance owners, escalation, revision, and retirement. The rejected clustering challenger cannot change the transparent community-review comparison.

## Session 4: Planning council recommendation and defense, 4 hours

The clinician lead chairs a 16-question defense with population-health, community, equity, epidemiology, GIS, access, implementation, model-risk, privacy, accessibility, and governance challenges. The panel records conditions, disagreement, curriculum-package status, fictional planning recommendation, and final-checkpoint permission separately.

If the designated clinician is unavailable, the program may use an approved recorded case conference plus a qualified clinician-led synchronous defense that preserves the same evidence, questions, challenge standard, and feedback.
