# Population and place claim boundary

The accepted population is an adult teaching population represented through area-level public and synthetic evidence. Its measures retain numerator, denominator, period, geography, uncertainty, support, suppression, and source role.

The map links 1,597 modeled PLACES estimates to valid tract geometries. 23 geometry-only tracts remain unavailable. Unavailable is not zero, low need, no concern, or not applicable.

A PLACES tract estimate is an area-level modeled value. It is not an observed local case count, diagnosis, resident characteristic, causal place effect, community identity, or service entitlement. ACS and SVI values do not describe each resident. County teaching aggregations do not become official county PLACES estimates.

Individual need or eligibility is prohibited. No address, resident, patient, household, organization, or participant may be inferred, ranked, contacted, or classified from an area value. The proposal stays at the level of fictional planning review.
