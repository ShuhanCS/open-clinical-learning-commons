# Community accountability and access

This classroom record does not represent observed community statements. The objections and incidents in the frozen package are synthetic tests. They show whether a proposed process preserves concerns; they do not show what any real community thinks.

Silence is not agreement. Attendance is not consent. Missing feedback is not acceptability. A later authorized review must preserve correction, objection, disagreement, refusal, withdrawal, referral, appeal, nonretaliation, pause, and stop routes.

Meaningful language and disability access must be designed before contact, not repaired after exclusion. Preparation materials, meetings, feedback routes, records, exact tables, map alternatives, and decision notices require accessible formats, interpretation or translation planning, plain language, and a named access owner.

Community representatives must have meaningful decision rights over the review process and a documented route to change or stop it. Compensation, burden, privacy, data minimization, record ownership, and return of findings require approval. No community contact is authorized by this module.
