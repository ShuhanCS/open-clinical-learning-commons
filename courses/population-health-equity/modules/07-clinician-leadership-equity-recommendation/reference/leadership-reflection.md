# Leadership reflection

The hardest leadership choice is not which tract ranks first. It is whether the evidence, purpose, rights, burden, access, and ownership are strong enough to justify asking affected people to spend time reviewing the idea.

The data reveal signals and constraints, but they cannot speak for communities. A transparent rule can still encode disputed values. A complete dry run can still be unready. A stable result under one perturbation can still fail under another. A technically accessible artifact can still exclude people if the process has no language, disability, compensation, or correction plan.

The selected recommendation preserves uncertainty and invites an authorized human body to decide whether bounded review is appropriate. It does not claim that review will produce approval. Leadership responsibility includes making disagreement visible, assigning owners, protecting stop rights, and being willing to retire the proposal.
