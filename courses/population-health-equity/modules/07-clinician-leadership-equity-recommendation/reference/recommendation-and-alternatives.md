# Recommendation and alternatives

## Separate decisions

- Curriculum package status: `accept with conditions`.
- Fictional planning recommendation: `recommend seeking approval for bounded structured community review`.

The package is complete enough for assessment because the accepted checkpoints, limits, conditions, owners, communication, and defense remain explicit. The proposal is not ready for implementation.

## Alternatives

1. `recommend seeking approval for bounded structured community review`: selected. It preserves a human process for testing the problem, evidence, access, burdens, rights, and alternatives before an implementation proposal exists.
2. `revise before seeking structured community review approval`: not selected because review should help shape the problem and evidence, but it becomes required if meaningful community authority, compensation planning, access, correction, recourse, or stop rights cannot be proposed.
3. `refer`: not selected as the primary class. Specific methods, civil-rights, privacy, causal, access, funding, and jurisdiction questions still require referral to their owners.
4. `stop`: not selected now because the classroom evidence supports asking whether a bounded review is appropriate. Stop becomes required if community authority, access, correction, monitoring, or accountability cannot be made real, or if area evidence is used for individual action.

## Boundary

No outreach, allocation, service, or implementation is authorized. The recommendation does not contact anyone, spend funds, select participants, approve a program, or establish benefit. A later authorized human body may revise, refer, or stop the review proposal.
