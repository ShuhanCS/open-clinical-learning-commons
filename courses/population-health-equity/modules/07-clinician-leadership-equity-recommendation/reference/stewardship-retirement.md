# Stewardship and retirement

Named humans retain every decision. No learner, model, analyst, clinician, or agent may determine real need, infer eligibility, speak for a community, allocate a resource, start outreach, deliver a service, estimate an effect, close a condition, or authorize implementation.

Stewardship covers the population and denominator, sources, geography, rate and disparity methods, suppression, targeting rules, review design, access, intervention assumptions, measures, triggers, incidents, evaluation, model risk, privacy, accessibility, and released version. Every material change requires a new release and renewed review.

Silence is not agreement. A condition remains open until its named human verifier reviews the required evidence. A synthetic objection cannot be closed as if a community approved the response.

Retire the proposal when no defensible purpose or accountable owner remains, review authority cannot be made meaningful, access or correction cannot be supported, evidence is used for individual inference, monitoring or stop rights are unavailable, burden cannot be justified, or unresolved harm cannot be addressed.
