# Population intervention brief

## Purpose

The candidate is a fictional voluntary diabetes-prevention access and navigation program. The planning question is whether a later authorized council should consider a bounded structured community review before any implementation proposal is developed.

The evidence covers adults represented through accepted Massachusetts area-level teaching data. It carries 28 carried tracts and 280 fictional planning places from one transparent comparison. No person is classified or contacted. A tract estimate does not identify a resident, establish diabetes, prove need, create eligibility, or substitute for consent.

## Proposed next step

Recommend seeking approval for bounded structured community review. The later approval request would need a defined purpose, authorized partners, compensation planning, language and disability access, correction, objection, disagreement, recourse, nonretaliation, pause, stop, documentation, and data-minimization rules.

## Nonaction and prohibited action

The next step is not outreach, enrollment, funding, allocation, service delivery, implementation, or evaluation. No organization or community has been contacted or has endorsed this proposal. The intervention remains not ready for real use, and outcomes remain unavailable.
