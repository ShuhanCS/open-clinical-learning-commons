# Standardization interpretation

## Direct method

The common standard is the matched ACS adult population across all 1,597 measure tracts. Its five weights are:

| Age band | Standard population | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| 18-34 | 1,681,962 | 0.2961321660 |
| 35-49 | 1,330,444 | 0.2342426662 |
| 50-64 | 1,409,112 | 0.2480932320 |
| 65-74 | 739,218 | 0.1301493300 |
| 75+ | 519,032 | 0.0913826058 |

The weights total one. For each tract, the direct calculation multiplies each available synthetic age-specific rate by the matching common weight and sums the five products. It produces a relative teaching index under one standard age distribution. It is not the tract's actual risk, event count, or program need.

Direct rates are available for 1,576 tracts. They remain unavailable for 21 tracts with at least one zero age-band denominator. The method does not silently replace an unavailable age-specific rate with zero.

## Indirect method

The complete statewide synthetic age-specific rates are applied to each tract's own age distribution to calculate expected synthetic events. The synthetic event count divided by that expectation produces a standardized event ratio.

Eighty tracts have an age-band denominator below 50 and must complete the guided indirect exercise. The indirect result is also available as a secondary check for the other tracts.

An indirect ratio is not directly comparable across tracts as though all tracts used the same standard weights. It does not establish excess real cases, disparity, causation, priority, eligibility, or funding need.
