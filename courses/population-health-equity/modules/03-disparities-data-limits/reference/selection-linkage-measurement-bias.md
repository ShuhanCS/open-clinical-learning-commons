# Selection, linkage, and measurement bias

The eight-row `bias-register.csv` keeps the source limitations attached to the analysis.

Selection begins before the missingness table. Module 03 receives only the 1,597 tracts in the accepted three-source measure intersection. Twenty-three ACS tracts in the 1,620-tract union do not enter the measure layer. Records without usable tract geography are outside the conditioned analytic universe, so zero missing geography cannot establish complete capture.

Linkage is deterministic at the accepted tract and age-band keys, but a correct join cannot repair selection. Every synthetic margin reconciles to the accepted denominator and generated numerator. That proves mechanical consistency, not whether real people or communities would have been captured.

Measurement is fixed by the generator. Combined race and ethnicity, primary language, and disability status are separate marginal categories with generated shares, risk multipliers, and missing groups. They do not measure self-identification, biology, access need, lived experience, or within-group diversity. The margins cannot be crossed.

Intervals quantify generated binomial variation under one accepted age standard. They omit ACS covariance, source-model uncertainty, category misclassification, generator uncertainty, and uncertainty from excluded records. The reference statement therefore stays inside the fictional release.
