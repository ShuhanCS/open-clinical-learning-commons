# Sensitivity analysis

The release runs 20 predeclared variants. Eighteen fill the requested award count. Two expose a resource shortfall: the community-review rule can fill 38 of 42 expanded awards under its three-per-county limit, and 26 of 28 awards under a two-per-county limit.

The equal geographic rule is highly dependent on its arbitrary tie breaker. Reversing the tie direction retains none of the 28 base selections. Removing the county quota retains 15, replaces 13, and reduces coverage from 14 counties to nine.

The need-based lower-confidence-limit variant retains all 28 base selections. Restricting the pool to supported display rows retains only two and replaces 26. The apparent choice therefore depends sharply on how the classroom support flag is handled.

The tested capacity minimum and access-ready conditions retain all 28 capacity-aware selections. That stability is local to this generated source and fixed ordering. It is not evidence that a real capacity rule would be stable or fair.

Allowing community rows marked `needs_revision` retains 19 base selections and changes nine. The stricter two-per-county limit produces a two-award shortfall. Review-state and concentration assumptions therefore change both who is included and whether the fictional resource can be assigned.

Resource count, support treatment, county limits, tie breaks, and review-state definitions change the result. That dependence blocks automatic use. Module 06 may carry one rule forward only as a documented fictional planning candidate with human review and recourse.
