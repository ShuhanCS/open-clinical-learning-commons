# Access and capacity review

The equal geographic rule covers every county but selects 11 language-access gaps, nine disability-access gaps, nine high-travel rows, six high-burden rows, three staff-not-ready rows, two objections, and 53 unresolved questions. Its selected fictional capacity is 580 places.

The need-based rule selects seven language-access gaps, eight disability-access gaps, seven high-travel rows, nine high-burden rows, 11 staff-not-ready rows, one objection, and 42 unresolved questions. Its selected fictional capacity is 710 places. High modeled prevalence does not repair delivery conditions.

The capacity-aware rule selects 1,120 fictional capacity places. It has no selected access-plan gaps, high-burden rows, staff-not-ready rows, or limited-support rows. It still has six high-travel rows and one unresolved objection, and it reaches only eight counties.

The community-review rule requires ready fictional review, both access plans, at least ten capacity places, and no unresolved objection. It selects 800 fictional capacity places. Twelve selected rows have high travel, five are staff-not-ready, and one has high burden. Those conditions must be repaired or monitored before Module 06 can describe a feasible test.

Capacity, access, staff, review, objection, and burden are generated teaching conditions. They are not observations about a real service system or community. None can create eligibility or authority.
