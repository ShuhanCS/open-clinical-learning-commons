# Accountable owner record

- Accountable decision owner: `FMA-DP-01 fictional resource-allocation owner`.
- Evidence steward: preserves the accepted Module 04 handoff, source labels, intervals, support states, suppression, and synthetic separation.
- Access owner: verifies language, disability, travel, notice, and appeal design.
- Community-review owner: records questions, objections, corrections, disagreement, and rereview without calling a generated state consent.
- Resource owner: owns the fictional 280-place constraint, ten-place award, capacity assumptions, and balancing consequences.
- Independent verifier: reruns source, rule, consequence, sensitivity, score, gate, and workspace checks.
- Escalation owner: pauses unresolved conditions and stops prohibited real or production use.

No owner may delegate fairness, community authority, or the final planning recommendation to a rule, map, modeled prevalence value, SVI rank, composite score, optimization routine, or AI system. Module 06 may use the community-review comparator to frame a fictional intervention design. A human remains responsible for every condition, disagreement, pause, and stop decision.
