# Assessment

## Decision

Which, if any, transparent fictional targeting rule is responsible enough to enter an intervention plan?

## Submission

Submit the complete targeting and fairness workspace. It must include the exact rule assignments and consequences plus all 17 learner records. The assignment is worth 15 points and counts once in the separate 25-point Week 6 checkpoint.

## Scoring

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Decision, resource, and rule integrity | 3 |
| Inclusion, exclusion, access, capacity, and burden accounting | 4 |
| Fairness, geography, group, benefit, harm, and sensitivity reasoning | 5 |
| Community review, recourse, reproducibility, AI, and claim limits | 3 |
| Total | 15 |

The numeric passing score is 12. A noncompensable gate failure overrides the score.

## Required evidence

The submission must preserve the accepted Module 04 handoff and the deterministic fictional planning source. It must show all four rules, all 6,388 rule-tract assignments, every inclusion and exclusion reason, the 280-place resource reconciliation for each rule, county concentration, suppression-preserving group consequences, pairwise overlap, all 20 sensitivity variants, benefit and harm, access and burden, review and recourse, accountable ownership, responsible claims, AI use, reproduction, score, gates, and progression.

No rule may be called fair without naming its specific definition and tradeoff. No selected row may be described as a real need, priority, eligible area, outreach destination, funding recipient, service location, consenting community, or implementation decision.
