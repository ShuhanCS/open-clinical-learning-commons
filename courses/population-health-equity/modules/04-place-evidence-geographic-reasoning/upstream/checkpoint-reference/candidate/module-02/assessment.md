# Assessment: population measure and denominator build

The Module 02 submission is worth 20 course points. Five criteria are worth four points each.

| Criterion | Points | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Population, age-band, numerator, denominator, and linkage logic | 4 | Exact source identities, 1,620-row union audit, 1,597 eligible tracts, 38-cell crosswalk, denominator logic, and unmatched-state accounting |
| Crude and age-specific rates | 4 | Exact numerator, denominator, multiplier, intervals, unavailable states, and reconciliation for all 7,985 tract-age rows |
| Direct and indirect standardization | 4 | Declared five-row standard, weights totaling one, direct calculation, expected counts, standardized event ratio, and method limits |
| Uncertainty, support, and evidence separation | 4 | ACS margin handling, Wilson intervals, zero and small-denominator states, 21 unavailable direct rates, 80 guided indirect cases, and separate PLACES evidence |
| Reproducibility, interpretation, and handoff | 4 | Executable SQL, independent Python checks, AI disclosure, gate record, conditions, and bounded Module 03 progression |

All 15 gates are noncompensable. A score cannot offset a changed source, hidden unmatched tract, denominator mismatch, unavailable-to-zero conversion, public-synthetic blend, unsupported observed-case claim, missing standard weights, failed reproduction, or expanded authority.

The score records technical and interpretive completion. It does not turn the synthetic numerator into a real result or approve any population-health action.
