# Context memo for the responsible teaching map

## Decision question

Can the accepted population evidence be communicated geographically without a wrong join, unstable support, ecological inference, inaccessible output, or language that treats a place or its residents as the problem?

## What the release establishes

The official 2024 TIGER/Line Massachusetts tract archive contains 1,620 unique tract geometries. All geometry checks pass. The exact tract-key join matches 1,597 accepted CDC PLACES modeled diabetes prevalence rows and retains 23 geometry-only tracts as unavailable.

The assessed SVG uses declared absolute classes. It retains the public estimate, model interval, population field, release label, support state, and claim limit in a complete exact table. The map is accessible through an embedded title and description, named non-color states, hatching, legend counts, an exact CSV, and a structured text alternative.

## What the pattern does not establish

The mapped values are modeled small-area prevalence estimates. They are not observed case counts, individual diagnoses, causal effects, program outcomes, or proof that every resident shares an area estimate. The boundary does not define a lived community, service area, clinical catchment, or decision constituency.

The map does not identify a priority tract, eligible population, outreach list, funding destination, allocation rule, intervention, or responsible actor. A dark color is not a statement that residents or places are deficient. Gray is unavailable, not zero. Hatching is a classroom review flag, not a CDC quality judgment.

## Small-area and aggregation limits

Forty-nine estimates trigger the classroom support review because of interval width, the retained PLACES population field, or both. The estimates remain visible with their exact intervals. The flag asks the reader to slow down; it does not decide whether the estimate is valid or invalid.

The 14 county teaching summaries are calculated from tract estimates and are not official county PLACES estimates. Six hundred forty-five tracts change display class when compared with the county summary class. This demonstrates the modifiable areal unit problem: the geographic unit changes the displayed pattern and the question being answered. Neither view supports individual inference.

## Language and accountability

Responsible language names the public source, modeled estimate, period, geography, interval, unavailable state, support rule, aggregation method, and intended reader. It avoids labeling a place as high need, vulnerable, deficient, noncompliant, failing, or priority based on a color.

Before any later fictional targeting comparison, human reviewers must ask whether the geography matches the decision, who is absent from the data, what access and capacity evidence is missing, who bears burden, who may benefit or be harmed, how community members can question the framing, and who can pause or stop the work.

## Decision

The reference map, exact table, text alternative, and context memo pass the Module 04 teaching boundary. Module 05 curriculum construction may compare transparent fictional rules only after freezing this release. No tract is prioritized, selected, contacted, made eligible, funded, allocated, implemented, or deployed.
