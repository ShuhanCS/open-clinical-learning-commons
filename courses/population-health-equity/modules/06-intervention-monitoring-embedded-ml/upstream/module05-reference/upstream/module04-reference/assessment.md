# APP-5 Module 04 assessment

## Submission

Submit a reproducible place-evidence release containing:

1. the verified source and checkpoint identities;
2. complete geometry, CRS, projected-area, key, join, and unavailable-state audits;
3. the declared map classes and classroom support rule;
4. tract and county aggregation outputs with exact interpretation limits;
5. one responsible accessible SVG map;
6. the complete 1,620-row exact map table;
7. a structured text alternative and context memo;
8. the ecological and contextual claims audit;
9. the three reviewed SQL files;
10. the score, gates, progression decision, reproduction check, and AI record.

The submission is worth 10 course points and counts once in the Week 6 checkpoint.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Source and geometry integrity | 2 | Exact official archive and manifest; complete geometry inventory; unique keys; CRS; types; validity; projected-area check |
| Join, aggregation, and stability accounting | 3 | Exact matched and unavailable states; retained intervals and population fields; declared support rule; county teaching summaries; aggregation comparison |
| Responsible accessible map and exact alternative | 3 | Fixed absolute classes; unavailable and support encodings; accessible SVG structure; source note; complete exact table; structured text alternative |
| Ecological, contextual, reproducibility, and claim limits | 2 | Claims audit; non-stigmatizing context memo; deterministic rebuild; AI accountability; bounded progression |

The numeric passing threshold is 8 of 10. A noncompensable gate failure overrides the score.

## Noncompensable failures

Any of these results in revision or referral regardless of points:

- changed checkpoint or TIGER source identity;
- missing, duplicate, malformed, wrong-state, null, empty, or invalid geometry;
- wrong or unreported CRS;
- a hidden join loss or unavailable value converted to zero;
- public PLACES and fictional synthetic evidence merged into one claim;
- missing estimate interval, source release, or support state;
- rank-based classes or a map color used as priority, need, eligibility, or target;
- county teaching summaries presented as official county PLACES estimates;
- inaccessible SVG, missing exact table, or missing text alternative;
- ecological, compositional, causal, stigmatizing, individual, or community inference;
- fabricated community input or undisclosed agent use;
- nondeterministic output or failed check; or
- implementation, production, or deployment authority.

## Reference result

The reference earns 10 of 10 and passes all 22 gates. Its disposition is `continue with conditions`. Module 05 curriculum construction may begin. No real-world action is authorized.
