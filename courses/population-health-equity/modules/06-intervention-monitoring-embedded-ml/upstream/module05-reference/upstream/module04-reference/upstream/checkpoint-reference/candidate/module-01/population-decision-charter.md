# Population decision charter

- Case ID: `FMA-DP-01`.
- Case status: `explicitly fictional Massachusetts adult diabetes-prevention planning review`.
- Decision: determine whether the population, denominator roles, geography, time frame, evidence roles, community-review rights, and accountability are coherent enough to begin population-measure construction.
- Population: adults age 18 and older represented by the accepted Massachusetts census tracts in the CDC PLACES 2025 `DIABETES` release, with each later measure retaining its own source-specific population and denominator.
- Provisional numerator concept: adults represented by a modeled diagnosed-diabetes prevalence or by a separately labeled synthetic event count in later instruction; Module 01 accepts neither as an observed local case count.
- Denominator roles: the PLACES adult population travels with the modeled PLACES estimate; ACS B01001 supplies separate 2020-2024 age-by-sex population estimates and margins for later denominator and standardization work; SVI population fields remain context and are not substituted into a health rate.
- Primary geography: Massachusetts census tract using an 11-character tract FIPS.
- Later comparison geography: county aggregation for a modifiable-areal-unit exercise after geometry and aggregation gates pass.
- Prohibited geography claim: no tract value may be assigned to an individual resident or treated as a complete community identity.
- Evidence periods: PLACES measure year 2023 in its 2025 release; ACS 2020-2024 five-year estimates; SVI 2022 with documented source vintages; TIGER 2024 geography reserved for Module 04.
- Surveillance cadence: review accepted source releases and compatibility at least annually for the fictional plan; do not construct a trend from incompatible releases or boundaries.
- Decision owner: the fictional `FMA-DP-01` population-health planning council.
- Affected communities: residents of represented tracts and the community organizations that serve or represent them.
- Intended next action: build and validate population, denominator, rate, and standardization measures in Module 02 for curriculum construction.
- Alternative action: revise the population, denominator, source, geography, or community-review contract before measure construction.
- Nonaction: stop or refer the question if the evidence roles, community authority, or potential harm cannot be bounded.
- Possible benefit: a transparent technical foundation for asking affected communities whether a fictional intervention-planning question deserves further work.
- Possible harm: stigmatizing places, mislabeling modeled values as observed disease, hiding missing tracts, assigning area values to people, or allowing an analytic output to drive allocation without community and human review.
- Required community role: question the framing, add local knowledge, contest interpretations and burdens, require revision, and stop progression to any real-world action.
- Authority boundary: no rate, disparity, map, tract rank, target, allocation, model, intervention effect, outreach, implementation, or policy is accepted in Module 01.

The charter supports structured community review as a future fictional step. It does not identify a tract for funding, represent a real Massachusetts agency or program, or grant authority over any real community.
