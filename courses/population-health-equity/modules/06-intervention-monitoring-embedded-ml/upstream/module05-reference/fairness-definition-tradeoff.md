# Fairness definition and tradeoff memo

## Equal geographic rule

This rule defines fairness as two teaching awards in every county. It reaches all 14 counties and is easy to reproduce. It also selects 11 language-access gaps, nine disability-access gaps, nine high-travel rows, six high-burden rows, three staff-not-ready rows, and two unresolved objections. Equal county representation does not make the selected areas equally ready or equally supported.

## Need-based rule

This rule defines fairness as greater fictional attention to higher modeled area-level prevalence. It selects 28 areas in seven counties, with a selected mean of 20.9928571429 percent. Twenty-six selected rows carry the classroom limited-support flag. The rule also selects 11 staff-not-ready rows, seven language-access gaps, eight disability-access gaps, nine high-burden rows, and one unresolved objection. A modeled prevalence estimate can frame a question, but it cannot act as an automatic need or allocation score.

## Capacity-aware rule

This rule defines fairness as feasible fictional delivery. Its selected rows contain 1,120 fictional capacity places and no language-access, disability-access, high-burden, staff-not-ready, or limited-support flags. The awards cover eight counties, six selected rows have high travel, and one has an unresolved objection. The rule can reward places that already have capacity while leaving lower-capacity places behind.

## Community-review rule

This rule defines fairness as procedural readiness with access, objection, recourse, and county concentration limits. It selects 28 areas across 11 counties. Every selected row has ready fictional review, both access plans, capacity of at least ten, and no unresolved objection. Twelve selected rows have high travel, five are staff-not-ready, and one has high burden. A ready review record does not prove consent, benefit, feasibility, or fairness.

## Decision

Carry the community-review rule into Module 06 as the least unacceptable fictional planning candidate. The rule remains conditional on human review, staff and travel repair, group-support review, accessible notice, appeal, correction, pause, and stop routes. It cannot select a real tract, person, community, service, or funding destination.
