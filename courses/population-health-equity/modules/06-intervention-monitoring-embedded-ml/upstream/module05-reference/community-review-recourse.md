# Community review and recourse

The community-review rule uses generated teaching states. `ready_for_planning_review` means only that a fictional record passes the declared classroom filter. It is not consent, endorsement, representation, or evidence that residents were asked.

Module 06 must design an accessible notice and review process before describing any prospective test. The process must support language access, disability access, correction of wrong data, objection, appeal, reconsideration, and a nonaction alternative. A person or community must be able to decline without losing unrelated services.

The fictional community-review owner receives objections and requests for correction. The access owner verifies language and disability access. The resource owner tests capacity and travel assumptions. The evidence steward preserves source and synthetic separation. The accountable owner records disagreement, decides whether to pause, and stops the exercise if a fictional result is represented as real authority.

Pause when review, access, staff, travel, capacity, group support, burden, or appeal routes are unresolved. Stop when a rule is used to contact a person or community, determine eligibility, direct money or services, claim consent, implement a program, connect to production, or deploy a decision system.
