# Instructor notes

Start with the uncomfortable result: the package is technically complete, but the intervention remains unready and the ML challenger fails. Ask learners why those outcomes demonstrate a working governance design rather than a failed assignment.

Keep public and fictional evidence separate. CDC PLACES estimates describe modeled area context. The resource layer, intervention, dry-run records, and access or staffing conditions are fictional. No learner should talk about a synthetic test record as a participant or a selected tract as a consenting community.

Use the six triggered measures to practice response ownership. A threshold creates human review, correction, pause, or stop. It never creates automatic contact, eligibility, allocation, enrollment, service, punishment, or model retraining.

Do not let learners tune the cluster analysis. The alternate seeds are strong, but the scaling median and selected-area coverage fail. The accepted conclusion is that the challenger is not useful. A learner may propose a future analysis, but cannot change this release after seeing its result.

The evaluation proposal is prospective only. APP-6 owns causal identification and effect estimation. Module 07 owns the clinician leadership review and final recommendation after the separate Week 6 checkpoint.

Reference workspaces contain answers and should not be distributed as learner starters.
