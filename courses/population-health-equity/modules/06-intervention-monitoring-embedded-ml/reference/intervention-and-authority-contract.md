# Intervention and authority contract

## Candidate

The candidate is a fictional voluntary diabetes-prevention access and navigation program. It would provide accessible information, preserve refusal and withdrawal, offer navigation, and test accessible group, remote, or hybrid support. The 28 carried tracts and 280 fictional places are a classroom planning constraint, not a real allocation.

## Intended population

The intended population is adults represented through area-level planning evidence. The design does not identify, contact, classify, or determine eligibility for any person. A tract estimate cannot stand in for individual need or consent.

## Human authority

Named people retain every decision. Program operations owns staffing and scheduling. Language and disability access owners control corrections. The community accountability owner controls feedback and recourse. Safety and privacy owners investigate incidents. The program steward may pause, revise, stop, or retire the design.

The transparent community-review comparison remains the starting point. The clustering challenger cannot change it. No threshold or model may trigger automatic outreach, eligibility, enrollment, allocation, service, punishment, implementation, or deployment.

## Current disposition

The package is complete enough for Week 6 checkpoint review. The fictional intervention is not ready for real implementation. Five staff-not-ready areas, twelve high-travel areas, one high-burden area, six monitoring triggers, and twenty-three dry-run incidents remain visible for owner review.
