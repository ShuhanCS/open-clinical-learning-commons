# Assessment

## Decision

Is the fictional intervention and monitoring design complete enough for Week 6 checkpoint review, and does the fixed clustering challenger add a bounded descriptive use without taking decision authority?

## Submission

Submit the complete learner workspace, including all 21 records and four SQL files. Module 06 adds no points. It is a required gate that carries the already accepted 25 points into the separate Week 6 checkpoint without counting them again.

## Required evidence

The submission must preserve the intervention, intended population, authority, theory of change, delivery pathway, access and capacity conditions, all 20 measures, every dry-run result, benefit and harm, feedback and recourse, incident escalation, pause and stop rights, later evaluation proposal, fixed clustering model card, stability and support findings, responsible claims, reproduction record, AI record, and progression decision.

The learner must accept the observed challenger result. Changing features, transformations, cluster count, seed, scaling variants, or thresholds after seeing the result fails the model-integrity gate.

## Gates

All 34 gates are noncompensable. A package can pass while the fictional intervention remains unready for real implementation and while the ML challenger is rejected. That distinction is a required finding, not a contradiction.
