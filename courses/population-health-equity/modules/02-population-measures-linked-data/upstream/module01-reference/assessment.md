## Noncompensable gates

All 12 gates must pass:

1. the accepted PLACES, ACS, and SVI raw and released identities match;
2. source rows, fields, tract keys, state filters, measure filters, releases, periods, and uncertainty fields reproduce;
3. the 1,597-tract three-source intersection and every unmatched tract state remain explicit;
4. PLACES, ACS, SVI, TIGER, and future synthetic intervention data have separate evidence roles;
5. population, numerator concept, denominator roles, geography, time frame, cadence, and accountable decision are explicit;
6. equity, disparity, and inequity are distinguished, and a measured difference is not automatically labeled unjust;
7. affected communities can question, require revision, and stop progression to real-world action;
8. modeled prevalence, area context, and relative ranks are not called observed cases, individual traits, causal effects, or program outcomes;
9. no tract is ranked, targeted, funded, excluded, stigmatized, or assigned an intervention;
10. no rate, standardization, disparity metric, map, targeting score, model, or intervention-effect estimate appears early;
11. AI use is disclosed, protected data remain prohibited, and human accountability is explicit; and
12. progression permits Module 02 construction only while real allocation, outreach, implementation, evaluation, and deployment remain prohibited.

## Progression decisions

- `continue`: every gate passes and no material condition remains.
- `continue with conditions`: every gate passes and named methods, source, community, clinical, or governance reviews remain open.
- `revise`: one or more required records or boundaries need repair before Module 02.
- `refer`: the population question, public-source use, community authority, privacy, or program decision requires a different owner.

The reference decision is `continue with conditions` for curriculum construction. It is not permission to calculate a real local rate, rank or target a tract, allocate resources, contact a community, implement a program, estimate an effect, or deploy a system.
