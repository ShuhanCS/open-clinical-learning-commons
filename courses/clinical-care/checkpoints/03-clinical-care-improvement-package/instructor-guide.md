# Final checkpoint instructor guide

- Verify the official section and half-term end date from the published calendar.
- Validate the complete candidate before reviewing its leadership judgment.
- Keep package disposition separate from clinical recommendation.
- The reference package passes because it is honest and complete, not because it approves a test.
- Require exact evidence paths for material claims.
- Conduct all 12 defense questions and at least one follow-up challenge.
- Record condition owners and escalation triggers.
- Do not create the proposed tag until named human approval, independent reproduction, and exact-commit verification are complete.
- Course acceptance does not authorize clinical implementation, model deployment, or patient targeting.
