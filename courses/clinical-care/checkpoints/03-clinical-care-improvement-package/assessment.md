# Final checkpoint assessment

The checkpoint records the Module 07 draft once as the final 35-point course component.

| ID | Criterion | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| C01 | Correct evidence, decision, and claim boundary | 8.00 |
| R01 | Reproducible evidence, package, and handoff | 5.00 |
| L01 | Sound clinical reasoning, equity, safety, and feasibility | 10.00 |
| A01 | Clear and action-guiding recommendation, ownership, and monitoring | 8.00 |
| H01 | Responsible agent use, accessibility, and defense | 4.00 |
| Total |  | 35.00 |

Passing requires at least 28.00 points, all 24 final gates, an adequate defense, `accept` or `accept with conditions`, and one allowed clinical recommendation. Numeric strength cannot waive changed evidence, a failed gate, an unsupported claim, inaccessible evidence, incomplete safety rules, hidden agent use, or absent ownership.

APP-1 totals 100 points: 20 at Week 3, 45 at Week 6, and 35 at the official half-term end.
