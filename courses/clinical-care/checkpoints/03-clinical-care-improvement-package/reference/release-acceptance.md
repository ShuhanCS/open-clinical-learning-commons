# Release acceptance and handoff

## Receiver gets

- exact accepted Week 3 and Week 6 packages;
- the complete Module 07 leadership candidate;
- evidence synthesis and technical appendix;
- a bounded recommendation of `revise before testing`;
- people, equity, safety, workflow, stakeholder, measure, and stop-rule records;
- evidence, accessibility, reproduction, agent-use, defense, and reviewer records; and
- six open conditions with owners and escalation triggers.

## Allowed use

Curriculum construction, named program review, learner instruction, and controlled assessment preparation.

## Prohibited use

Clinical implementation, model deployment, patient targeting, real site or group ranking, and claims of causation, efficacy, fairness, quality, access, safety, or real-population performance.

## Change and stop rule

Any changed candidate byte requires a new Module 07 version and renewed final review. A prohibited-data finding, failed validator, changed release identity, inadequate defense, unsupported claim, missing owner, or unauthorized tag stops acceptance.

## Tag status

`app1-clinical-care-candidate-v0.1.0` is proposed and not created. Named human approval, independent reproduction, learner defense, and exact-commit verification are required before authorization.
