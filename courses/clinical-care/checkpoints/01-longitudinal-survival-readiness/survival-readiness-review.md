# Survival-readiness review

The accepted pathway begins with 518 index people. Nine index deaths, eight early post-discharge deaths, and 25 early acute returns are explicit nonoverlapping branches. The corrected day-30 landmark cohort contains 476 people, including 129 with a recorded scheduled follow-up encounter and 347 without one.

The survival package conserves 87 first later acute returns and 389 administrative censors. The log-rank p-value is 0.67258471, which does not prove equivalence. The unadjusted Cox hazard ratio is 1.10542457, but the Schoenfeld residual time screen fails at p = 0.00636020. Count-first Kaplan-Meier and fixed-time evidence therefore remain the main survival summary.

Three later deaths occur after first later acute return. Zero pre-event death censors does not prove independent censoring. The supplied R script remains pending until executed in a named managed environment.

Decision: the package may enter Module 04 with an open survival-methods condition. Module 04 must preserve the risk set, define case mix before adjustment, and may not hide the failed PH screen behind one constant adjusted hazard ratio.
