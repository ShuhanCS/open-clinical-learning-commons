# Instructor notes

The Week 6 checkpoint is the complete technical case, not a new assignment. Score Module 04 at 25 points and Module 05 at 20 points. Module 06 is a required gate and adds no points.

Ask learners to explain the chain in plain language: the survival limitation, the risk-adjusted comparison, the care-pattern measurement question, what the retrospective source cannot say about equity, the proposed prospective workflow, and why the random forest does not change the decision.

The reference disposition is `continue with conditions`. Module 07 leadership may narrow, defer, refer, or stop the proposal. It cannot use a perfect score to erase open reviewer conditions or authorize clinical implementation.
