# Module 01 readiness assessment

The module uses a 20-point readiness rubric. These are feedback points, not additional course points. Accepted evidence enters the cumulative 20-point Week 3 checkpoint.

| ID | Criterion | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| D01 | Decision owner, target population, action, and pathway logic | 4 |
| T01 | Exposure, comparator, timing, landmark, outcomes, and claim boundary | 4 |
| F01 | Exact source-feasibility interpretation and site-sparsity condition | 4 |
| S01 | Patient, clinical, analytic, operational, and governance stakeholder reasoning | 4 |
| R01 | Reproducible package, access, agent accountability, and progression decision | 4 |
| Total |  | 20 |

Passing requires at least 16 points and all 12 gates:

1. exact source course and Synthea fingerprints;
2. exact 16-table source inventory and totals;
3. exact 518/8/25/485 cohort flow;
4. exact 129/87/25/62 exposure and outcome facts;
5. 64 source organizations and `not ready` raw site comparison;
6. discharge, exposure window, landmark, and outcome window are distinct;
7. early death and acute return remain visible;
8. outcome set includes primary, process, balancing, safety, access, and patient-important concepts;
9. no causal, clinical-efficacy, real-population, fairness, or site-ranking claim;
10. no patient, restricted, credential, secret, or personal-path data;
11. complete accessible records and agent accountability; and
12. complete conditions plus an allowed progression decision.

The reference progression is `continue with conditions`.
