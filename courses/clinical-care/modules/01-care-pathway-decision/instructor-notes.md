# Instructor notes

## Teaching emphasis

Keep the decision ahead of the method. Learners should leave knowing why the day-30 landmark exists, why early events remain visible, which outcomes matter, what the source can support, and what must wait for later modules.

Do not calculate or reward a crude follow-up effect in Module 01. The exposure is post-discharge, the source is synthetic, and adjustment is not yet built.

## Review judgment

The validator confirms structure and exact evidence. The instructor decides whether the question is clinically coherent, the outcome set matters, the evidence standard is actionable, stakeholders are credible, and the learner understands why the analysis supports a prospective test rather than an effectiveness claim.
