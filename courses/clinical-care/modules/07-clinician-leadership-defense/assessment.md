# Module 07 assessment

Module 07 drafts the final 35-point clinical care improvement component. Checkpoint 3 records the reviewed score once as the course grade.

| ID | Criterion | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| C01 | Correct evidence, decision, and claim boundary | 8.00 |
| R01 | Reproducible evidence, package, and handoff | 5.00 |
| L01 | Sound clinical reasoning, equity, safety, and feasibility | 10.00 |
| A01 | Clear and action-guiding recommendation, ownership, and monitoring | 8.00 |
| H01 | Responsible agent use, accessibility, and defense | 4.00 |
| Total |  | 35.00 |

Construction review requires at least 28.00 points, all 24 gates, an adequate 12-question defense, and an allowed clinical recommendation. The score evaluates the quality and honesty of the package. It does not reward approval of a test, a favorable outcome, or model use.

The reference draft is 35.00 of 35.00 with a clinical recommendation of `revise before testing`.

2. Checkpoint 2 identity and 113-file package are exact.

12. Every material leadership claim traces to exact evidence.
13. One allowed recommendation states its authorization boundary.
14. People, burden, exclusion, equity, and safety are addressed.
15. Stakeholder roles include accountable and responsible owners.
16. Workflow and capacity assumptions are explicit.
17. The bounded test has eligibility, comparison, duration, and a learning aim.
18. Required measure families and data contracts are complete.
19. Monitoring, reassessment, stop, rollback, and escalation rules are complete.
20. Exact tables and equivalent access routes are complete.
21. Clean validation and immutable evidence comparison pass.
22. Material agent use has an independent check and human owner.
23. All 12 defense answers are adequate.
24. Package status, recommendation, final-checkpoint permission, and prohibited use are separate and consistent.
