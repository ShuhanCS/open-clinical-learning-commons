# Clinician-led session plan

## Block purpose

The block teaches learners to move from accepted analysis to an accountable clinical leadership decision. It uses the continuing discharge follow-up case and the learner's own leadership package.

## Session 1: Clinical decision conference, 4 hours

- Read the accepted Week 3 and Week 6 decisions.
- Separate what the evidence shows from what the organization still needs to learn.
- Explain why the failed proportional-hazards screen changes the survival summary.
- Identify the medical director's decision, authority, and claim boundary.
- Draft the evidence synthesis.

Dr. Joseph leads the case conference and challenges the clinical meaning of each claim.

## Session 2: People, workflow, and stakeholder lab, 4 hours

- Identify patients, caregivers, clinicians, schedulers, and leaders affected by the proposal.
- Map benefit, burden, exclusion, safety, access, and equity questions.
- Assign accountable, responsible, consulted, and informed roles.
- Test workflow and capacity assumptions.
- Draft the people, stakeholder, and feasibility records.

Dr. Joseph leads the tradeoff and ownership review. A patient or caregiver partner and operations lead should join when available.

## Session 3: Recommendation and monitoring studio, 4 hours

- Choose one allowed recommendation.
- Define a bounded prospective test without model targeting.
- Define process, outcome, balancing, access, implementation, and data-quality measures.
- Set reassessment, pause, stop, rollback, and escalation rules.
- Draft the care-improvement council brief.

Dr. Joseph challenges the recommendation, operational assumptions, and safety rules.

## Session 4: Council defense and feedback, 4 hours

- Deliver the accessible evidence handoff.
- Answer all 12 defense questions.
- Respond to one clinical, one operational, one methods, and one patient-impact challenge.
- Record conditions, owners, recommendation, and final-checkpoint permission separately.

Dr. Joseph chairs the clinical defense and records clinical conditions. Faculty record the course score and technical conditions.

## Equivalent route

If a live conference is unavailable, the course may use an approved recorded case conference plus a synchronous defense led by a qualified hospital medicine clinician. The same evidence, questions, follow-up challenges, and assessment standard apply.
