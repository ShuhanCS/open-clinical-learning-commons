# Clinical care improvement recommendation

- Recommendation: `revise before testing`.
- Decision owner: `hospital medicine medical director or care-improvement council role`.
- Proposed reach: `universal offer to locally eligible discharges; no model targeting`.
- Current authorization: `curriculum construction only`.
- Clinical implementation: `prohibited`.
- Model deployment: `prohibited`.

## Recommendation

Retain the question of whether a universal discharge follow-up scheduling offer is feasible, acceptable, and measurable. Revise the proposal with local workflow and capacity evidence before requesting authorization for a bounded test.

Before review for a live test, the organization must name clinical and operational owners, map the current discharge workflow, document scheduling capacity, define local eligibility and exclusions, involve a patient or caregiver partner, and add prospective fields for offer, preference, acceptance, appointment status, completion, barriers, and burden.

The proposal must use universal offer and measurement. It must not use the transparent benchmark or random forest to decide who receives an offer. The accepted model comparison does not establish clinical utility and does not change the improvement decision.

## Evidence needed for the next decision

- local baseline and denominator feasibility;
- workflow and staffing capacity;
- patient and caregiver review;
- approved process, outcome, balancing, access, implementation, and data-quality measures;
- privacy and governance approval;
- stop, rollback, escalation, and restart authority; and
- a written protocol that matches the reviewed workflow.
