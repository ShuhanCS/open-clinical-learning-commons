# Workflow and feasibility review

## Current state

The accepted retrospective source identifies recorded scheduled follow-up but does not reveal whether an offer occurred, whether a patient wanted or accepted it, whether an appointment was available, or whether it was completed. A local current-state map is therefore required.

## Proposed state for design review

1. Confirm local eligibility and exclusions before discharge.
2. Offer follow-up without using a model score.
3. Record preference, acceptance, refusal, and preferred format.
4. Check appointment capacity and record the scheduling result.
5. Provide the patient and receiving practice with the confirmed plan.
6. Record completion, cancellation, no-show, barriers, burden, and exceptions.
7. Review process, access, workload, safety, and data quality at the declared cadence.

## Capacity questions

- How many eligible discharges occur per day and by service?
- How many appointment slots are available within the defined window?
- Who performs scheduling and exception handling?
- How much time does each case require?
- What happens when the preferred practice or format is unavailable?
- How are language, disability, transportation, technology, and caregiver needs supported?

## Feasibility conclusion

The retrospective source cannot answer these questions. Local workflow observation and capacity data are required before test authorization. The proposal remains `revise before testing`.
