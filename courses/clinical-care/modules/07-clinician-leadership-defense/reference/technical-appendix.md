## Claim limits

The source is synthetic. The site extension is synthetic and known by construction. Risk adjustment does not remove residual confounding. Subgroup evidence is suppressed when support fails. Offer, preference, acceptance, appointment status, completion, barriers, and burden require prospective collection.

The package supports curriculum review of a bounded proposal. It does not establish causation, clinical benefit, quality, access, equity, site performance, fairness, safety, or deployment value in a real population.
