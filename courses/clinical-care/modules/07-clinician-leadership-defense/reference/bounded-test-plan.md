# Bounded prospective test plan

- Status: `design proposal; not authorized`.
- Setting: `one locally approved hospital medicine service or unit`.
- Reach: `universal offer to locally eligible discharges`.
- Targeting model: `none`.
- Comparison: `approved baseline or phased comparison defined before collection`.
- Initial duration: `four weeks after a two-week workflow and data-quality run-in`.
- Review cadence: `weekly during run-in and test`.
- Final authority: `named hospital medicine medical director or council`.

## Learning aim

Determine whether the organization can deliver and document a universal follow-up scheduling offer with acceptable patient choice, access, workload, safety, and data completeness. The test is not designed to prove that follow-up causes fewer acute returns.

## Eligibility and exclusions

Local leaders must define eligibility, exclusions, service boundary, discharge setting, follow-up window, and exception rules before authorization. The accepted synthetic cohort is a teaching case, not a local eligibility list.

## Required prospective states

Offer, preference, acceptance or refusal, appointment availability, scheduled status, completion, cancellation or no-show, barriers, burden, and exception reason.

## Authorization boundary

This plan is a curriculum example. No site, clinician, patient, workflow, or data collection may be enrolled from this package. Local clinical, operational, patient-partner, privacy, governance, and methods approval is required.
