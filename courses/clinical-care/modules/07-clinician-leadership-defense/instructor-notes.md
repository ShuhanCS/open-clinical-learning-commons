# Instructor notes

- Treat the two accepted checkpoint packages as immutable evidence.
- Keep package quality separate from the clinical recommendation.
- Do not reward a learner for approving a test. Reward a defensible, bounded decision.
- The reference answer is `revise before testing` because local workflow, capacity, prospective measures, patient-partner review, and governance approval are missing.
- Model-based targeting is outside scope. The bounded random forest does not change the decision.
- Carry the failed proportional-hazards condition into the defense.
- Require exact evidence paths when a learner states a number.
- Ask who has authority to authorize, pause, stop, and restart the work.
- A pending clinician schedule or reviewer name is a pre-alpha condition, not evidence that the reference package is incomplete.
- The final checkpoint records the 35-point score once.
