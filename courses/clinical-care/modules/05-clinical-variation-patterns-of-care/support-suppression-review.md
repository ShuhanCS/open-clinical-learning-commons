# Support, suppression, and accessible display review

## Teaching-site support

The recorded day-30 follow-up comparison keeps the fixed SITE-A through SITE-F order. Every site has at least 50 people, at least 10 people with a recorded follow-up, and at least 10 without one, so all six have the status `report with caution`.

The observed counts are SITE-A 19 of 76, SITE-B 19 of 75, SITE-C 19 of 68, SITE-D 21 of 88, SITE-E 20 of 87, and SITE-F 31 of 82. They are synthetic labels with known direct effect zero. The fixed order is not a ranking; no best, worst, high-performing, low-performing, or league-table language is permitted.

The post-landmark any-scheduled-care measure is not used as a six-site comparison because four sites have fewer than 10 people in the complement. Day-30 medication reconciliation among the 129 recorded-follow-up people is also not compared by site because every site has fewer than 10 numerator records. These suppressions prevent a sparse table from becoming a ranking.
