# Assessment

Module 06 is a required Week 6 gate and awards no additional course points. The cumulative checkpoint scores Module 04 at 25 points and Module 05 at 20 points exactly once.

## Gates

1. Exact accepted Module 02, Module 04, and Module 05 identities and fingerprints.
2. One-to-one conservation of 476 people and 87 outcomes.
3. Prespecified age, source-recorded gender, race, and ethnicity groups in fixed order.
4. Counts and field missingness reported before subgroup estimates.
5. Process and outcome suppression rules applied without merging small groups.
6. No source-record result is called proof of access, fairness, discrimination, or inequity.
7. Observed pathway counts reconcile exactly.
8. Unobserved offer, preference, scheduling, completion, barrier, and burden states remain explicit.
9. Pathway figure has an accessible title, description, exact tables, and structured alternative.
10. Driver diagram connects the aim, drivers, and candidate changes.
11. Measure registry includes implementation, process, outcome, access, safety, and balancing measures.
12. Improvement proposal remains a bounded prospective test for Module 07 review.
13. Both models use the same prediction time, four eligible features, split, rows, and threshold.
14. Prohibited, demographic, site, exposure, and post-prediction fields never enter either model.
15. The evaluation set is not used for fitting, tuning, model search, or threshold selection.
16. Brier score, AUC, log loss, calibration, confusion counts, and paired uncertainty are reported.
17. Error costs are labeled an educational scenario, not a clinical value judgment.
18. Subgroup model results are suppressed when evaluation support is insufficient.
19. Every false negative and aggregate false-positive burden are reviewed.
20. Machine learning does not replace the transparent benchmark or prospective workflow measurement.
21. No clinical implementation, group-specific action, real-site ranking, or deployment claim is made.
22. Two builds, learner/reference assembly, copied validation, and mutation rejection pass.
23. AI use has material-claim verification and an accountable human owner.
24. The Week 6 checkpoint assigns 45 points exactly once and records a valid Module 07 progression decision.
