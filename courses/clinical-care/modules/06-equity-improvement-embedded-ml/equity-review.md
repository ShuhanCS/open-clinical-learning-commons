# Equity review

## Prespecified question

In a prospective scheduling workflow, do offer, preference, appointment status, completion, barriers, and burden differ across the fixed age, source-recorded gender, race, and ethnicity groups after support, missingness, and privacy review?

The retrospective proxy is a qualifying scheduled-follow-up record by day 30. It does not establish offer, access, attendance, preference, completion, quality, need, benefit, or burden.

## Counts, missingness, and support

All 476 accepted people have source values for age, gender, race, and ethnicity. Zero recorded missingness is not proof that the categories are complete, accurate, or meaningful identities. The source gender field contains only F and M and does not establish gender identity.

## Interpretation

The synthetic summaries identify questions for prospective measurement, not evidence that any group received unfair care. The source categories cannot establish lived identity, access barriers, discrimination, fairness, or inequity. The fixed group order is not a ranking.

- Equity conclusion: `question retained`
- Proof of unfairness: `not supported`
- Prospective requirement: `collect offer, preference, appointment status, completion, barrier, burden, and safe-escalation states with the same prespecified support rules`

The improvement proposal should be universal at eligibility screening. Subgroup summaries are for monitoring and learning, not different thresholds or different care.
