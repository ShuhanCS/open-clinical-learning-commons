# Instructor notes

## Teaching purpose

Module 06 asks learners to finish the technical case without letting a complex model become the case. Start with the missing equity and access states, move to a feasible prospective workflow, and introduce the random forest only after the question, features, split, threshold, and error costs are frozen.

## Discussion prompts

1. What can a scheduled-follow-up record tell us, and what can it never tell us?
2. Why do suppressed groups remain visible instead of being combined?
3. What prospective fields would let the team study offer, preference, completion, and burden?
4. Is catching three additional events worth 32 additional false positives in this teaching scenario?
5. Why does neither answer support model deployment or real clinical implementation?
