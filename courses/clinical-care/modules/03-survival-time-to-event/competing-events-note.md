# Death and censoring note

Three landmark-eligible people have a later recorded death. Each death occurs after that person's first later acute return, so all three people are counted as events before death. No recorded death censors a person before the event.

The remaining 389 no-event people are administratively censored at the fixed day-335 boundary. They contribute event-free time and remain in their group risk set until that boundary.

Zero observed pre-event death censors does not prove independent censoring. The source is synthetic, death is date-granular, and unrecorded loss to follow-up is not represented. Real competing outcomes or doubtful follow-up would require a survival analyst and clinician to reconsider the estimand.
