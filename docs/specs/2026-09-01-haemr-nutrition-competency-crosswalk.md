# HAEMR Nutrition Competency Crosswalk

- Status: working selection for institutional and nutrition review
- Date: 2026-09-01
- Commons release: `0.101.0`
- Proposed setting: Harvard-Affiliated Emergency Medicine Residency
- Proposed track: NIH Developing Track, Residency Program
- Official framework: https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/nutrition-competencies-framework.pdf

## Decision

Use 16 HHS competencies across six domains to define a 40-hour emergency medicine residency pathway. Treat this as the working selection until HAEMR completes a baseline curriculum inventory and a qualified nutrition professional reviews the clinical content.

The framework recommends assessing the existing curriculum before selecting competencies and supports a minimum 40-hour equivalent. This crosswalk therefore uses the official competency numbers and hour equivalents, but selects the competencies for emergency medicine rather than assuming every program has the same gaps.

## Selection rules

Each selected competency must:

1. change an emergency physician's assessment, communication, consultation, referral, transition, or evidence judgment;
2. fit physician scope and preserve clear roles for registered dietitians and other nutrition professionals;
3. produce observable resident work that can be assessed;
4. support a curriculum another residency can teach without protected health information or proprietary software; and
5. contribute once, and only once, to the 40-hour total.

Applied analytics, visualization, simulation, and responsible AI are teaching methods within these components. They do not create extra hours unless they teach and assess a selected HHS competency.

## Approval gates before submission

- HAEMR curriculum leadership confirms the current gaps, resident years, schedule, required or elective status, and feasible instructional hours.
- A qualified nutrition professional verifies the competency interpretation, evidence sources, clinical accuracy, and scope boundaries.
- Division AI confirms the data, visualization, simulation, and AI-audit activities it can build and maintain.
- The Health Data Analytics collaborator confirms its role in analytic methods, visualization, reproducibility, or assessment.
- The writing team updates this crosswalk and the 15-page Curriculum Overview together so the competency numbers, hours, activities, and assessments remain identical.

Competencies not selected here remain available for later expansion. They should not be claimed in the submission unless the curriculum gives them explicit time, instruction, and assessment.
