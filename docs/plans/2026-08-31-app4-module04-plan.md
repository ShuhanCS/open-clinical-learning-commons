# APP-4 Module 04 build plan

## Purpose

Build the runnable APP-4 Module 04 package for alert burden, human factors, access, equity, privacy, and human override. The module must carry the accepted Week 3 release forward unchanged, compare every unaccepted evidence threshold under an interruptive design and a less interruptive design, include no alert, and permit only a bounded Module 05 sandbox build.

## Fixed upstream contract

- Accept only Checkpoint 01 version `0.1.0` at Commons `0.80.0`.
- Preserve the 245-row checkpoint candidate manifest with SHA-256 `4e78d2313ce324fd372e6fc187afee333b27ed0cc0270c6ab8c08354dd5c3151`.
- Verify all 204 nested immutable rows and the complete 263-file reference checkpoint.
- Preserve the six unaccepted evidence candidates: `0.02`, `0.03`, `0.04`, `0.05`, `0.075`, and `0.10`.
- Preserve `0.20` as a rejected Module 02 mechanics fixture.
- Do not change accepted source, logic, model, calibration, score, gate, progression, or authority evidence.

## Module data contract

1. Reuse the complete accepted Synthea FHIR release through the checkpoint chain of custody.
2. Build a deterministic fictional `CGH-GIM-01` workflow overlay with 1,000 synthetic people and repeat encounter opportunities.
3. Calculate offline teaching scores only for synthetic records with ready inputs, using the fixed Module 03 coefficients without refitting.
4. Preserve missing, stale, and inconsistent inputs as unavailable states. Do not impute them or treat them as below threshold.
5. Compare all six candidates under an interruptive banner and a passive contextual panel, then compare no alert.
6. Report eligible encounters, candidate cards, cards per clinician session, repeats, unavailable inputs, scripted response times, views, dismissals, deferments, acknowledgments, and unresolved cases.
7. Label all workflow and interaction values as scripted synthetic teaching data. A dismissal cannot be interpreted as fatigue, misuse, or poor care.
8. Audit age, source-recorded gender, language access, disability access, and BMI slices with explicit support suppression.
9. Keep public NHANES threshold consequences separate from synthetic workflow counts.

## Learner records and assessment

The package will require records for task analysis, roles and handoffs, timing and interruption, burden assumptions, candidate comparison, usability, automation-bias controls, access and equity, patient communication, override and stop conditions, the workflow release, a 25-point score, gates, reproduction, AI use, progression, and the Module 05 handoff.

The 25 points are counted once at the Week 6 checkpoint. Progression requires every noncompensable gate. A passing reference may choose one design as a sandbox fixture, but no threshold becomes clinically accepted and no real-patient scoring, clinical alerting, clinical action, implementation, production connection, or deployment authority is granted.

- The workflow builder reproduces exact outputs and refuses overwrite.
- The workspace builder produces byte-identical learner and reference immutable manifests and refuses overwrite.
- The validator checks upstream identity, nested manifests, generated evidence, all records, the exact score, gates, progression, and authority boundaries.
- Self-checks reject copied learner work, immutable mutations, missing candidates, the `0.20` fixture as evidence, unsupported equity estimates, failed gates, altered score, unauthorized progression, and clinical or deployment claims.
- The central curriculum checker validates the 21-section durable specification and complete package.
- The complete curriculum regression passes before commit and push.
