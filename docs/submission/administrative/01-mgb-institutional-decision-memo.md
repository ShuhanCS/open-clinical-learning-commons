# Institutional decision memo

Date: September 1, 2026

To: Mass General Brigham Graduate Medical Education, Research Management, Department of Emergency Medicine leadership, and authorized institutional officials

From: Shuhan He, MD, Emergency Medicine, Massachusetts General Hospital; Program Director, Healthcare Data Analytics, Mass General Brigham University

Subject: Expedited approval request for an NIH Nutrition Education Challenge submission through HAEMR

Requested response: As soon as possible, preferably by September 4, 2026

## Decision requested

Approve preparation and submission of one Mass General Brigham entry to the NIH Integration of Nutrition Training into Health Care Education Challenge, Developing Track, Residency Program category, using the Harvard Affiliated Emergency Medicine Residency as the educational setting.

The recommended administrative structure is a single-Entity submission by Mass General Brigham. The MGB Department of Emergency Medicine Division of Artificial Intelligence would build and coordinate the curriculum. The Mass General Brigham University Healthcare Data Analytics Program would serve as an internal academic collaborator, not a formal Partner Entity, unless Research Management or legal counsel directs otherwise.

## Why Mass General Brigham is the working Lead Entity

The current AMA FREIDA record identifies Mass General Brigham as the sponsor of the four-year emergency medicine residency, ACGME program 1102421150. The current NRMP directory also identifies the institution as Mass General Brigham. NIH permits a fully accredited, United States-based nonprofit academic institution that provides an ACGME-accredited residency program to enter the Residency Program category.

Research Management should confirm the exact legal entity name and address required on the official Participant Agreement. The public program record is strong evidence for the route, but it does not replace institutional certification.

## Competition facts

- Sponsor: National Institutes of Health Office of Nutrition Research.
- Competition: Integration of Nutrition Training into Health Care Education Challenge.
- Track and category: Developing Track, Residency Program.
- Federal deadline: September 15, 2026, 11:59 PM Eastern Time.
- Prize: up to $75,000 for each selected Developing Track residency entry.
- Submission type: federal prize competition, not a grant, cooperative agreement, contract, or subaward.
- Application budget: none.
- Required final package: Curriculum Overview PDF, Nutrition Education Toolkit PDF, and signed Participant Agreement PDF.
- Entry limit: one entry per institution, whether submitted alone or in a formal partnership.
- Public-use term: participating entities grant NIH an irrevocable, paid-up, royalty-free, nonexclusive worldwide license to reproduce, publish, post, link to, share, and publicly display the submission for challenge purposes. The participating entities retain their other applicable intellectual property rights.
- Funding restriction: federal grant, cooperative agreement, or other transaction funds may not be used to develop or support the submission.

## Proposed curriculum

The working title is Open Nutrition Data Commons for Emergency Medicine Residency. The proposed curriculum is a 40-hour, longitudinal nutrition data and responsible AI pathway for HAEMR residents. It currently maps 16 HHS competencies across six domains to emergency medicine learning activities and observable assessments.

The curriculum would use synthetic patient cases and open health data. Public adopters would not need MGB credentials, protected health information, MGB Azure access, or proprietary software. The proposal does not include patient-facing AI, clinical deployment, effectiveness claims, or use of real patient records. Any later educational evaluation or use of institutional data would follow normal MGB review and approval processes.

- Mass General Brigham: Lead Entity, institutional applicant, prize recipient if selected, and owner of the institutional certifications.

- Healthcare Data Analytics Program: applied analytics, visualization, reproducibility, and assessment design.
- Qualified nutrition professional: clinical nutrition content review and scope verification. This person remains to be named.
