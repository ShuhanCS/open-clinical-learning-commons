NIH Integration of Nutrition Training into Health Care Education Challenge

Re: Healthcare Data Analytics collaboration for Open Nutrition Data Commons for Emergency Medicine Residency

To the Challenge Reviewers:

The Mass General Brigham University Healthcare Data Analytics Program supports the proposed Developing Track curriculum, Open Nutrition Data Commons for Emergency Medicine Residency.

The program's role is to connect nutrition education with practical data reasoning. Subject to university approval, Healthcare Data Analytics will contribute:

- applied analytic methods for interpreting nutrition, clinical, and population data;
- clear and accessible data visualization activities;
- reproducible workflows using synthetic cases and appropriately licensed open data;
- assessment methods for source selection, data interpretation, uncertainty, and communication; and
- faculty review of the curriculum's analytic workload and adoption materials.

These contributions will support the HHS competencies selected by HAEMR and the curriculum team. Analytics and visualization are teaching methods within the 40-hour map and will not be counted as additional nutrition hours without a mapped competency and assessment.

Healthcare Data Analytics is an academic collaborator in the proposed Mass General Brigham submission. This letter does not designate Mass General Brigham University as a formal Partner Entity, make it a claimant to prize funds, or authorize submission on the university's behalf. Any change to that status requires separate institutional approval.
