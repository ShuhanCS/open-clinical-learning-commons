## Decision requested

Authorize Mass General Brigham to submit one Developing Track residency entry to the NIH Integration of Nutrition Training into Health Care Education Challenge through the Harvard Affiliated Emergency Medicine Residency.

The recommended structure is a single-Entity submission by Mass General Brigham. The Mass General Brigham University Healthcare Data Analytics Program is the proposed curriculum creator and academic lead within the MGB submission, not a formal Partner Entity, unless Research Management or legal counsel directs otherwise. The Department of Emergency Medicine is the proposed institutional sponsor and implementation partner.

Every role, activity, purchase, and implementation detail remains conditional on institutional review. The proposed operating model allows Healthcare Data Analytics to purchase food for scheduled nutrition learning sessions through the institutional route MGB designates. The team will revise the curriculum and operating plan to follow the institutional rules and approval path identified by the responsible offices.
