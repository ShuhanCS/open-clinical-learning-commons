## 13. Cumulative records, reproducibility, AI, privacy, and claims

### Source record

The source record names all three module releases, Synthea source and dictionary URLs, analytic-table fingerprint, synthetic status, rights boundary, and allowed teaching use.

### Transformation record

The transformation record distinguishes upstream module transformations from checkpoint assembly. Assembly copies registered bytes, writes a manifest and summary, and adds cumulative records. It does not clean, impute, calculate, round, redraw, or edit accepted evidence.

### Reproducibility record

The record includes platform, Python, module validator results, assembler and checkpoint validator results, artifact count, manifest fingerprint, existing-target refusal, incomplete-submission rejection, and known operating-system limits.

### AI-use record

The record names tool, model, purpose, data shared, advice used, affected files, human decision, and at least one material verification against a source row, rule, denominator, interval, figure table, contrast calculation, or reproduction hash.

AI output is not evidence by itself.

### Accessibility, privacy, and claims

- Accessibility is a noncompensable gate.
- Only synthetic data are permitted.
- No identity-like field is newly exposed by checkpoint assembly.
- Small internal teaching cells remain exact but are not a universal disclosure precedent.
- No real clinical, operational, population, performance, safety, access, equity, utilization, cost, trend, forecast, or causal conclusion is supported.
