## 13. Recommendation, alternatives, and review proposal

The recommendation record separates two judgments:

- Is the curriculum package complete enough to assess and carry forward?
- What should the fictional council recommend as the next planning step?

The alternatives analysis must address all four allowed classes. It explains why immediate stopping is not selected, why unbounded progression is prohibited, what revision would be required if review governance is absent, and what questions require referral.

The bounded review proposal must name purpose, participants, decision rights, compensation planning, language and disability access, preparation materials, data minimization, correction, objection, disagreement, recourse, documentation, nonretaliation, pause, stop, reporting, and retirement. Module 07 describes these requirements but does not recruit, contact, convene, fund, approve, or operate a review.

Community review is not a ceremonial approval step. It may change the problem, reject the proposed targeting frame, revise the evidence needed, identify harms not visible in the data, or stop the proposal. Silence, attendance, or missing feedback is not agreement.
