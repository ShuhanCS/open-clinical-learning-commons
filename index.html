Module 04 / Distributions versus summaries

One number says the emergency department improved.

Look at the patients it leaves out. This five minute investigation uses 8,392 synthetic visits. You do not need an account or coding experience.

The brief

Make the first call.

The fast-track pathway was designed to move lower acuity patients through the department more quickly. Leadership has one result and a proposal.

Leadership note

Median length of stay fell from 186 to 117 minutes.
"The new pathway is working. We should expand fast track across the department."

Would you support the proposal from this result alone?

The evidence

Change the measure. Change the story.

Each measure is correct. Select all four and notice which patients each one describes.

Median length of stay

A typical visit became much shorter across 2026.

37% lower

The median supports leadership's success story, but it describes the middle visit rather than the longest waits.

Where the visits went

The middle moved left. A second group stretched far right.

These bars show the share of visits in each length of stay range. December has many more short visits and a larger group beyond eight hours.

Your recommendation

What should leadership do next?

The fast-track pathway did help many discharged patients. The worsening tail points to a different operational problem.

Choose one.

A strong recommendation accounts for both the patients who moved faster and the patients whose waits became much longer.
